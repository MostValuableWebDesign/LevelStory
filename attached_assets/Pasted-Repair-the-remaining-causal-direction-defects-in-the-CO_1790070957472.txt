`evaluateOrbBreakPullbackContinuation()` currently treats this as sufficient:

```ts

It does not strictly verify:
- context.breakout.failed === false;
- an allowed current breakout state;
- breakout time at or before P;

- occurrence direction;
- occurrence ORB epoch;
- conflict between stored source and current evidence.
The stricter hasCausalContinuationDirection() is only used by Patience Candle Continuation.
A failed, stale, future, mismatched, or fabricated breakout source can therefore pass the ORB Pullback evaluator.
2. Missing ORB epoch identity fails open
