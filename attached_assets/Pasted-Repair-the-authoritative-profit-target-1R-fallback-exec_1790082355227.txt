Repair the authoritative profit-target, 1R fallback, execution, and P/L calculations for LevelStory continuation trades.

Review the current repository before editing because file contents may have changed. Implement this through the complete production chain. Do not patch only the chart or explanation text.

Confirmed defects

1. The target planner currently rejects forward key levels whose buffered target is below 1R.

This is incorrect. A valid forward key level or indicator has priority over the 1R fallback, even when that target produces less than 1R.

2. The current 1R fallback is calculated from entry to the buffered strategy stop.

This is incorrect. Profit-side 1R must be measured from the actual modeled entry fill to the raw adverse wick of the patience candle.

3. The fixed eight-tick near-side target placement is not applied consistently to every eligible key level.

Premarket, prior-session, ORB, NTZ, major levels, VWAP, EMA 200, and other governed target levels must use the same eight-tick near-side target placement.

4. The incorrect target flows into execution, trade outcome, P/L, Visual Review, and the generated explanation.

Authoritative target-selection rule

At the completed E snapshot, freeze the levels and indicators that were causally visible.

For each level in the profitable direction, measure the raw directional distance from entry to the nearest boundary that price would encounter.

The eligible search interval is:

```text
raw level distance > 5.00 points
raw level distance ≤ 20.00 points
Boundary rules:
- Exactly 5.00 points: skip.
- Less than 5.00 points: skip.
- More than 5.00 points: potentially eligible.
- Exactly 20.00 points: eligible.
- More than 20.00 points: skip.
Select the nearest eligible forward level after applying those boundaries.
Place the executable target eight MES ticks before the selected raw level:
MES tick size = 0.25
8 ticks = 2.00 points
Long:
target = selected forward level boundary − 2.00
Short:
target = selected forward level boundary + 2.00
Apply this placement to all eligible governed target types, including:
- EMA 200
- VWAP
- premarket high and low
- previous-session high and low
- two-sessions-ago high and low
- ORB high and low
- NTZ high and low
- major support and resistance
- governed Dynamite/confluence levels
Do not require the buffered key-level target to be at least 1R.
Remove key-level rejection based on:
TARGET_LEVEL_SKIPPED_BELOW_1R
A level may still be skipped because it is:
- on the wrong side of entry
- 5 points or less from entry
- more than 20 points from entry
- non-causal or unavailable at completed E
- diagnostic-only
- malformed or non-finite
- a prohibited level type such as Fibonacci or previous-session close
Add explicit reason codes such as:
TARGET_LEVEL_SKIPPED_WITHIN_5_POINTS
TARGET_LEVEL_SKIPPED_BEYOND_20_POINTS
Target priority
Use this order:
1. Nearest causally visible forward key level or indicator more than 5 and no more than 20 points from entry.
2. Executable target eight ticks before that level.
3. Patience-wick 1R fallback only when no eligible level exists in that interval.
Do not compare the key-level target against 1R to decide whether to keep it.
Authoritative 1R fallback
Use 1R only when there is no eligible forward level between more than 5 and no more than 20 points from entry.
Calculate fallback 1R from the actual modeled entry fill to the raw patience-candle adverse wick.
Long:
risk points = modeled entry fill − patience-candle low
1R target = modeled entry fill + risk points
Short:
risk points = patience-candle high − modeled entry fill
1R target = modeled entry fill − risk points
The raw patience wick is the candle’s actual high or low. Do not add or subtract the strategy-stop buffer.
The following must not determine profit-side 1R:
- buffered strategy stop
- primary-loss exit level
- catastrophe stop
- consolidation midpoint stop
- adaptive stop buffer
- account risk amount
- fixed dollar target
Fail closed when:
- the modeled entry fill is missing
- the patience candle is missing
- the required patience high or low is non-finite
- the patience wick is on the wrong side of entry
- calculated risk is zero or negative
Do not fall back to entry-to-stop 1R when patience-wick evidence is invalid.
Planning versus execution
The actual modeled entry fill may differ from the trigger threshold because of an entry gap or slippage.
Therefore:
- Key-level selection may use the governed candidate entry reference to select and freeze the level.
- Once the actual modeled fill is established, the authoritative 1R fallback must be calculated from that fill and the frozen raw patience wick.
- Candidate, trade, replay, audit, and Visual Review must store the same final fallback target.
- Do not let the target planner display threshold-based 1R while execution uses fill-based 1R.
- Do not let execution recompute 1R from the strategy stop.
If necessary, pass an explicit field into OHLCV execution, such as:
oneRRiskAnchorPrice
oneRRiskAnchorType = PATIENCE_WICK
Then calculate oneRPrice after the modeled fill is known.
