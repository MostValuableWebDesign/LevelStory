

Phase 7 — Futures risk, targets and runners
Use XHigh or Max effort.
Implement only Phase 7 of LevelStory.

FUTURES POSITION SIZING

Calculate:

Stop distance in ticks =
absolute(entry price - catastrophe stop) ÷ tick size

Risk per contract =
(stop distance in ticks × dollar value per tick)
+ modeled entry slippage
+ modeled exit slippage
+ round-trip fees

Maximum contracts =
floor(maximum trade risk ÷ risk per contract)

Require whole contracts and always round down.

Block trades when maximum contracts equals zero.

RISK CONTROLS

Implement:

- Maximum risk per trade
- Maximum daily loss
- Maximum trades per day
- Maximum contracts
- No averaging down
- Duplicate-entry prevention
- Maximum spread
- Minimum liquidity
- Stale-data lockout
- Emergency kill switch

STOPS

Maintain:

1. Catastrophe stop
   - Hard protective stop
   - Always active

2. Strategy stop
   - Based on patience-candle or level invalidation
   - May trigger intrabar

PROFIT TARGETS

Require selection before entry:

- $50
- $75
- $100
- Custom between $50 and $100

Default shadow target:

$75

Convert dollars to valid ticks:

Required ticks =
ceiling(target dollars ÷ dollar value per tick)

MULTIPLE CONTRACTS

- Close at least one contract at the selected target
- Designate remaining contracts as runners

RUNNER

A runner begins after partial profit is taken.

Freeze its reference point when runner management begins.

Calculate adverse retracement from the most favorable price achieved.

Exit the runner intrabar when adverse retracement reaches or exceeds 40% of the measured runner impulse.

Do not continually move both Fibonacci anchors.

The catastrophe stop remains active.

SLIPPAGE

Initial model:

- Normal market: one adverse tick per fill
- Fast market: two adverse ticks
- Abnormal spread: spread plus adverse slippage

FEES

Include configurable:

- Commission
- Exchange fees
- Regulatory fees
- Clearing fees

Report gross P/L, slippage, fees, and net P/L.

Add tests for every calculation and lockout.
