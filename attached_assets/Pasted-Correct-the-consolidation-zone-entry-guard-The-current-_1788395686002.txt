Correct the consolidation-zone entry guard. The current implementation is too strict.

CURRENT INCORRECT BEHAVIOR

The implementation requires the entire entry candle range to be outside the frozen consolidation zone. Any wick touching or overlapping the zone causes rejection.

That is not my strategy.

AUTHORITATIVE RULE

An entry cannot occur inside an active frozen consolidation zone, but the complete E candle does not need to be outside it.

A valid immediate-next entry candle may:

- Open inside the consolidation zone.
- Trade through the consolidation boundary.
- Have a wick overlap or retest the boundary.
- Close outside the frozen zone in the trade direction.

Direction-aware requirements:

LONG

- E must be the immediate completed candle after P.
- E must reach the bullish patience confirmation threshold.
- E must close strictly above the frozen consolidation-zone high.
- The modeled entry/fill price must be above the consolidation-zone high.
- E may open inside the zone or have a lower wick inside it.

SHORT

- E must be the immediate completed candle after P.
- E must reach the bearish patience confirmation threshold.
- E must close strictly below the frozen consolidation-zone low.
- The modeled entry/fill price must be below the consolidation-zone low.
- E may open inside the zone or have an upper wick inside it.

INVALID CASES

Reject the entry when:

- E closes inside the frozen consolidation zone.
- E only wicks outside but closes back inside.
- The confirmation threshold is reached inside the zone but price does not reach a valid entry price outside it.
- The modeled fill would be inside or exactly on the consolidation boundary.
- E is not the immediate candle after P.
- The entry occurs inside NTZ or at/after 1:00 p.m. ET.

EFFECTIVE ENTRY THRESHOLD

The executable threshold must satisfy both the patience buffer and consolidation boundary:

For a long:

effectiveEntryThreshold =
  max(
    P.high + configuredConfirmationBuffer,
    consolidationZoneHigh + 1 MES tick
  )

For a short:

effectiveEntryThreshold =
  min(
    P.low - configuredConfirmationBuffer,
    consolidationZoneLow - 1 MES tick
  )

E must reach this effective threshold and close outside the zone in the correct direction.

Do not fabricate an entry when only the close condition or only the threshold condition passes.

CAUSAL SCOPE

- Apply this rule only to the active frozen consolidation zone associated with the candidate’s causal setup.
- Do not use stale, future, unrelated, or subsequently detected consolidation zones.
- An unrelated consolidation zone must not block an ORB or patience-continuation setup.
- Preserve the frozen zone identity, high, low, detection timestamp, breakout direction, P timestamp, E timestamp, and effective threshold in the audit record.
