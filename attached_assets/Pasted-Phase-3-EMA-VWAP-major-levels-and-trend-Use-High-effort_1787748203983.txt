MAJOR SUPPORT AND RESISTANCE

Analyze approximately one year of completed hourly candles.

A support candidate requires at least three distinct historical upward reactions from approximately the same price area.

A resistance candidate requires at least three distinct historical downward rejections from approximately the same area.

Merge nearby reactions into zones.

Maximum of:

- Two futures ticks

Make all thresholds configurable.

- Recency
- Reaction magnitude

CONFLUENCE

Classify:

- One component: normal level
- Two overlapping components: strong confluence
- Three or more components: dynamite level

Possible components:

- Hourly support/resistance
- Premarket high/low
- Yesterday’s high/low
- Two-days-ago high/low
- NTZ/ORB boundary
- VWAP
- 200 EMA
- Fibonacci level

15-MINUTE TREND

Use eight completed 15-minute candles initially.

Bullish evidence:

- Higher highs and higher lows
- Price above VWAP
- Price above 5-minute 200 EMA
- EMA flat-to-rising

Bearish evidence:

- Lower highs and lower lows
- Price below VWAP
- Price below 5-minute 200 EMA
- EMA flat-to-falling

Otherwise classify trend as neutral.

Display the evidence behind every classification.
