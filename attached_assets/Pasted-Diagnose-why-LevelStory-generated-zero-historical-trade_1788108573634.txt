AUTHORITATIVE PATTERN TO TRACE

For the ORB edge, trace this causal sequence:

1. The first three completed five-minute candles establish ORB/NTZ high and low.
2. Price completes a breakout above or below ORB/NTZ.
3. Price pulls back or retests a qualifying level within the governed 12-MES-tick zone.
4. A valid completed patience candle P forms.
5. Only the immediately following completed five-minute candle E may confirm P.
6. If E fails, P expires and scanning continues for a new P2 followed by E2.
7. A confirmed P→E pair is a trade candidate even without a modeled fill, risk approval, or finalized outcome.
