The target snapshot must represent the causal market state available when the immediate entry candle E completed.

  - A pre-E snapshot.
  - An open/incomplete-E snapshot.
  - A later audit cursor’s newly discovered levels.

- A later audit with a new closer level cannot change the frozen target.

- Repeated identical runs produce the same target snapshot and target price.
