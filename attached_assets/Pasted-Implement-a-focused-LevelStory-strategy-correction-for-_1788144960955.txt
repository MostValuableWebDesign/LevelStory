1. Mandatory outside-NTZ entry rule

Add this as authoritative strategy knowledge and durable project memory:

“No entry may be taken inside or on the boundary of the finalized NTZ/ORB range.”

The NTZ and ORB are the same finalized range: the high and low of the first three completed five-minute regular-session candles from 9:30–9:45 a.m. ET.

For every edge and every authoritative trade candidate:

- Long entry confirmation price must be strictly greater than NTZ/ORB high.
- Short entry confirmation price must be strictly less than NTZ/ORB low.
- Equality with either boundary is not outside and must not qualify.
- The completed E candle must reach both:
  - The P-candle confirmation threshold, and
  - A price strictly outside the finalized NTZ in the trade direction.
- Use the later/more restrictive threshold when these differ.
- The modeled entry price must never be inside the NTZ.
- Entries remain prohibited at or after 1:00 p.m. ET.
- Do not fabricate a breakout or infer one from future candles.

Apply this rule to all entry-producing edges:

- ORB break–pullback–patience continuation
- Patience candle continuation
- Strong breakout after consolidation
- Equivalent-candle reversal

A patience shape, pullback, consolidation, or reversal may still be recorded diagnostically inside the NTZ, but it cannot become an eligible trade candidate until its immediate E candle confirms outside the NTZ.

Add an explicit rejection/status reason such as:

ENTRY_NOT_OUTSIDE_FINALIZED_NTZ

Preserve immediate P→E behavior:

- Only the immediately following completed candle may confirm P.
- If E fails, P expires.
- Continue scanning for a later P2 followed by its own immediate E2.

2. Dynamite Level confluence

Add a governed setting:

dynamiteLevelToleranceTicks = 8

For MES:

- 8 ticks = 2.00 points.
- A Dynamite Level exists when at least two distinct, causally available level families fall inside one cluster whose total maximum-to-minimum width is no greater than eight ticks.
- Use deterministic clustering and MES tick rounding.

Eligible level families may include:

- ORB/NTZ boundary
- Premarket high/low
- Previous trading-day high/low
- Two-trading-days-ago high/low
- Causal VWAP value at the evaluated candle
- Causal 200 EMA value at the evaluated candle
- Confirmed support/resistance zone
- Valid causal Fibonacci retracement level

Do not inflate confluence by counting aliases or duplicated sources:

- ORB high and NTZ high are one level family/value, not two.
- ORB low and NTZ low are one level family/value.
- “Critical premarket high” and “premarket high” must not count twice if they are the same underlying level.
- Repeated audit references must not create additional confluences.

Persist for each Dynamite Level:

- Deterministic cluster ID
- Lower and upper price boundaries
- Representative price
- Included level IDs, types, values, and source families
- Number of distinct confluences
- Causal observation timestamp
- Whether the trade’s pullback interacted with the cluster

A Dynamite Level is:

- A stronger qualifying level/confluence
- Supporting evidence that increases the candidate grade
- Not a separate strategy or automatic entry
- Never a replacement for a valid P→immediate-E sequence
- Never permission to enter inside the NTZ

Display Dynamite Levels in royal blue on the chart:

- Use a royal-blue line or narrow shaded zone.
- Suggested color: #4169E1.
- Label it “Dynamite · N confluences” with its price or price range.
- Keep individual constituent levels available in details, but avoid unreadable duplicate lines and labels.

3. Tests

Add focused deterministic tests proving:

- Long entry at or below NTZ high is rejected.
- Short entry at or above NTZ low is rejected.
- Entry exactly on a boundary is rejected.
- A valid immediate E outside the NTZ qualifies.
- A failed E expires P and a later P2/E2 can qualify.
- No entry at or after 1:00 p.m. ET.
- Two distinct level families within eight ticks form one Dynamite Level.
- Levels more than eight ticks apart do not.
- ORB and NTZ aliases do not double-count.
- Repeated audit references do not double-count.
- Dynamite evidence increases grade but does not create a separate trade.
- One physical P→E signal still creates at most one candidate and one Shadow Mode trade.
