Complete Stage 4 of the LevelStory Visual Review redesign: responsive polish, accessibility, and end-to-end verification.

Preserve the accepted design and all functionality from Stages 1–3. Do not introduce another redesign or new trading features.

RESPONSIVE BEHAVIOR

Desktop:
- Maintain the trade navigator beside the chart.
- Keep trade identity and Previous/Next convenient while reviewing.
- Prevent overlapping sticky headers or obscured controls.

Tablet:
- Narrow or collapse the navigator while retaining clear access to trade selection.
- Keep the chart readable rather than squeezing every panel into columns.

Mobile:
- Use a single-column workspace with an accessible trade selector or trade-list drawer.
- Keep current trade identity and Previous/Next easy to reach.
- Preserve all chart, review, and evidence controls.
- Avoid horizontal page overflow.
- Keep chart-specific pan/zoom and fullscreen usable.

Check representative widths around 1440px, 1024px, 768px, and 390px, plus a typical laptop height.

ACCESSIBILITY AND POLISH

Verify:
- Semantic buttons, tabs, disclosures, and form labels.
- Visible keyboard focus and logical focus order.
- Accessible contrast.
- Status labels and symbols in addition to color.
- No essential information available only on hover.
- Appropriate touch targets.
- Reduced-motion support.
- Long strategy names, large values, and missing data do not break layouts.
- Drawers/dialogs manage focus correctly and return it to their trigger.

Correct spacing, overflow, excessive blank areas, text wrapping, and chart-control collisions.

REGRESSION CHECKLIST

Verify the actual application, not just screenshots or source-text tests:

Generation:
- Empty, running, completed, zero-candidate, failed, partial, cached, and stale states.
- Both loading indicators start together.
- Active-job ETA messaging remains visible.
- Existing non-increasing ETA behavior is preserved.
- Retry and refresh/resume behavior remain intact.

Trade navigation:
- Cross-date and cross-strategy selection.
- Correct filtered counts and queue position.
- First/last boundaries.
- Rapid selections never mix data from different trades.
- Unsaved-review protection on every selection path.
- Stable list scrolling and accessible chart navigation.

Review and chart:
- All chart controls, markers, causal boundaries, and candle locking.
- Correct authoritative prices, P/L, quantities, grades, and results.
- Review submission, saved status, error handling, and teaching controls.
- Exports, review ledger, and supporting panels.
- Chart Analysis and Account Impact remain functional.

Use saved results and isolated fixtures first. Do not rebuild the historical index or launch broad replays merely to validate styling.

DELIVERY

Run frontend type checking and relevant behavioral tests. Fix failures introduced by this redesign.

Rebuild and restart the preview where needed. Verify the actual preview URL serves the final build, not stale assets.

Provide:
1. A concise summary of the completed redesign.
2. Files changed.
3. Exact checks run and their results.
4. Any unverified behavior or remaining limitations.
5. Desktop and mobile screenshots using generated trades.
6. Confirmation that each original capability remains accessible.

Do not declare completion if interactions were not checked. Clearly distinguish verified behavior from assumptions.

The acceptance goal: a trader can select, inspect, judge, and advance through trades without repeatedly hunting for the chart, navigation, or review controls.
