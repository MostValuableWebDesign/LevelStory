AUTHORITATIVE PATIENCE-CANDLE RULES

Context:
- It forms after a qualified ORB breakout and pullback to or near a critical level, VWAP, EMA, Fibonacci level, or valid consolidation.
- The trend must agree with the intended trade.
- A patience candle is not required to be a complete inside candle.

Bullish patience candle:
- The 15-minute trend must be bullish.
- The candidate candle’s high must not exceed the previous completed candle’s high.
- The rest of the candle may extend outside the previous candle.

Bearish patience candle:
- The 15-minute trend must be bearish.
- The candidate candle’s low must not go below the previous completed candle’s low.
- The rest of the candle may extend outside the previous candle.

Trigger:
- Only the immediate next five-minute candle can trigger the setup.
- Long entry occurs only after price reaches 3–4 ticks above the bullish patience candle’s high.
- Short entry occurs only after price reaches 3–4 ticks below the bearish patience candle’s low.
- Use the configured four-tick default and the contract’s actual tick size.
- Crossing only the raw high/low without reaching the full buffer is not an entry.
- If the immediate next candle does not reach the buffer, expire the pattern. Later candles cannot reuse it.

Invalidation and stop:
- If the immediate next candle crosses the opposite patience extreme first, invalidate the setup.
- Long strategy stop: below the patience candle’s low plus the configured safety buffer.
- Short strategy stop: above the patience candle’s high plus the configured safety buffer.
- A catastrophe stop must remain separate from the strategy stop.
- If OHLC data shows both sides were crossed and event order cannot be proven, reject as AMBIGUOUS_EVENT_ORDER.
- Do not assume favorable event order.
