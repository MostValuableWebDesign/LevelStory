CURRENT VERIFIED DEFECTS

1. Equal-timestamp entry and stop evidence is silently discarded.

The executor filters post-entry points with:

`point.timestamp > modeledFillTimestamp`

When entry and stop observations have the same timestamp and no sub-order information, the stop point disappears. The result becomes an open/manual trade with no ambiguity.

Verified reproduction:

- Modeled fill: 105
- Exit reason: manual
- Ambiguity labels: empty
- Stop-hit timestamp: null
- Modeled exit timestamp: null
- Remaining quantity: 1

This must be an unresolved entry/exit sequence, not an authoritative open trade.

2. Strong replay writes and validates different frozen-zone identity formats.

Phase 9 currently stores:

`activeConsolidationZoneId`

when one exists, but replay validates against:

`zoneLow|zoneHigh|calculationVersion`

A valid production-generated Strong trade can therefore fail replay compatibility.

3. Production cache versions were not advanced after adding ordered replay evidence.

Authoritative trade output now contains `orderedExecutionEvidence`, but these production versions remain unchanged from the preceding implementation:

- Fixed formula: v23.19
- Strategy-result cache: v7
- Batch aggregation: v3
- Qualification funnel: v7
- Route execution management: v15

Old persisted trades without the new replay evidence can therefore load as current.

4. Ordered tick evidence is required too broadly.

The replay validator currently requires `orderedExecutionEvidence` for every Strong or Extended trade. Valid direct trades based entirely on deterministic OHLC behavior or an exact later gap-open exit may have no ordered tick evidence. Those trades become permanently unreplayable even after regeneration.
