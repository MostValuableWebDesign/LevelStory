Audit and correct LevelStory’s historical pullback and patience-candle detection. The historical MES charts contain numerous visible pullbacks within the configured 12-tick level zone and valid patience candles, but Visual Review is not finding them.

Primary objective

Detect and retain every causal pullback candidate and patience-candle occurrence independently of whether it ultimately becomes a qualified trade.

Do not require a completed entry or winning trade merely to recognize a pullback or patience candle.

Detection pipeline

Implement separate deterministic stages:

1. Level-interaction candidate
2. Pullback candidate
3. Patience-candle candidate
4. Immediate next-candle confirmation
5. Risk/formula approval
6. Qualified trade or rejected setup

A failure at a later stage must not erase evidence from an earlier stage.

Example:

- Price comes within 12 ticks of VWAP.
- Record a VWAP pullback candidate.
- A valid patience candle forms.
- Record the patience candle.
- The next candle fails confirmation.
- Do not create a trade, but retain the pullback and patience-candle occurrences with “entry confirmation failed.”

Authoritative level tolerance

MES tick size: 0.25
Default proximity: 12 ticks / 3.00 points

For every qualifying level V and completed five-minute candle C:

if V < C.low:
    distancePoints = C.low - V
else if V > C.high:
    distancePoints = V - C.high
else:
    distancePoints = 0

distanceTicks = distancePoints / 0.25

Level interaction qualifies when:

distanceTicks <= 12

Use the candle’s complete high–low range. Do not compare only its close.

Apply the same authoritative 12-tick default to:

- historical occurrence scanning;
- formula evaluation;
- Visual Review generation;
- frontend qualification controls;
- API validation;
- deterministic replay;
- Shadow Mode;
- backtest setup detection.

Search the codebase for remaining hard-coded values of 4 ticks, 1.00 point, `DEFAULT_LEVEL_TOLERANCE_TICKS = 4`, or equivalent comparisons.

Dynamic indicators

- Resolve VWAP and EMA 200 at the exact candle timestamp.
- Never use their final-session values.
- EMA 200 must use completed five-minute closes and sufficient causal warm-up history.
- Preserve fractional indicator precision.

Patience-candle detection

Evaluate each completed five-minute candle after a pullback or consolidation candidate.

Bullish patience candle:

- The active directional trend is bullish.
- The candle’s trend-direction top/high does not exceed the preceding completed candle’s top/high.
- The whole candle does not need to be inside the preceding candle.

Bearish patience candle:

- The active directional trend is bearish.
- The candle’s trend-direction bottom/low does not go below the preceding completed candle’s bottom/low.
- The whole candle does not need to be inside the preceding candle.

The patience candle must be recorded even if the next candle does not confirm an entry.

Entry confirmation remains separate:

- E must be the immediately following completed five-minute candle.
- Long: E confirms 3–4 ticks above P.
- Short: E confirms 3–4 ticks below P.
- A later candle cannot replace E.
- If E fails, preserve P and label the setup “patience found; next-candle confirmation failed.”
