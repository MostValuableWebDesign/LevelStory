Implement one narrow, compute-conscious correctness pass for the authoritative pullback-arm lifecycle in LevelStory.

Do not rebuild the historical index, run a broad backtest, modify the strategy definitions, change the 12-tick pullback tolerance, change the eight-tick P→E confirmation buffer, alter stops or profit targets, or add broker/live/paper execution.

Objective

Finish the Phase 4 → Phase 5 → Phase 9 pullback lifecycle so every breakout creates one deterministic, monotonic arm whose identity and state cannot regress across repeated audit evaluations.

Required corrections

1. Authoritative monotonic lifecycle

Create or use one canonical lifecycle record per pullback arm ID.

The state may only advance through valid transitions. Repeated audit cursors may observe an existing state, but must not recreate, duplicate, regress, or replace it.

Terminal states are immutable:

- CONSUMED
- STRUCTURALLY_INVALIDATED
- SUPERSEDED_BY_NEW_BREAKOUT
- OPPOSITE_BREAKOUT_INVALIDATED
- ENTRY_CUTOFF_EXPIRED
- SESSION_BOUNDARY_EXPIRED
- CONTRACT_BOUNDARY_EXPIRED

Once an arm reaches a terminal state:

- Never overwrite it with another state.
- Never emit another terminal transition.
- Never rearm or consume it again.
- Record conflicting later observations as diagnostics only.

Do not use last-write-wins state handling.

2. Make lifecycle ownership explicit

Phase 4 owns:

- ARMED_AFTER_BREAKOUT
- PULLBACK_OBSERVED
- LEVEL_INTERACTION_FOUND
- SUPERSEDED_BY_NEW_BREAKOUT
- OPPOSITE_BREAKOUT_INVALIDATED
- ENTRY_CUTOFF_EXPIRED
- SESSION_BOUNDARY_EXPIRED
- CONTRACT_BOUNDARY_EXPIRED
- Any governed Phase 4 structural invalidation

Phase 5 owns and must project back into the shared authoritative lifecycle:

- PATIENCE_ARMED
- SIGNAL_CONFIRMED
- CONSUMED
- Any governed patience/confirmation structural invalidation

Do not merely declare lifecycle states in a type union. Every exposed state must have an implemented transition and deterministic test. If `STRUCTURALLY_INVALIDATED` has no governed predicate, remove it from active lifecycle claims or report it explicitly as unsupported rather than fabricating a transition.

3. Preserve causal pullback behavior

Keep these rules unchanged:

- No six-candle or 30-minute eligibility expiration.
- Same-direction continuation remains part of the original breakout until a countertrend pullback begins.
- A newer completed same-direction breakout after the pullback begins supersedes the old arm and may create a new independent arm.
- A completed opposite-direction breakout invalidates the old arm.
- Date and contract boundaries expire the arm.
- E must open before the exclusive 1:00 p.m. ET cutoff.
- Pullback interaction still uses the governed 12-MES-tick range.
- P must be followed immediately by E.
- If E1 fails, P1 expires and scanning continues for P2.
- E2 may confirm only P2, never P1.
- A confirmed/consumed arm cannot produce another candidate.

4. Separate data-quality gaps from session boundaries

Do not classify a missing or non-contiguous intraday candle interval as `SESSION_BOUNDARY_EXPIRED`.

Add a truthful data-quality state or diagnostic, such as:

- DATA_GAP_INVALIDATED

Use it only when continuity required for causal evaluation is unavailable. Do not fabricate missing candles.

5. Correct status reporting

If a terminal boundary is found after earlier post-breakout candles, the arm must still report its terminal/expired status.

Do not let the existence of earlier observed candles cause a terminal arm to remain reported as merely `observed`.

6. Strengthen the late-pullback regression

Replace the current late-pullback fixture with one where:

- The first six candles and first 30 minutes do not touch or enter the qualifying level’s 12-tick zone.
- The first genuine qualifying interaction occurs only after those old diagnostic thresholds.
- The arm remains active.
- The later interaction is detected once.
- A later valid P→E sequence creates exactly one candidate.

The fixture must prove a genuinely late first interaction, not merely another interaction after earlier qualifying candles.

7. Add focused deterministic integration tests

Add tests covering:

A. Late interaction

- First qualifying pullback interaction occurs after six candles/30 minutes.
- It remains eligible and can create one candidate.

B. P1 failure and P2 success

- P1 forms.
- E1 fails immediate confirmation.
- P1 expires.
- P2 forms on the same active arm.
- E2 confirms P2.
- Exactly one candidate is created from P2→E2.

C. Consumption

- A confirmed arm becomes CONSUMED.
- Later audit evaluations cannot create a second candidate or change its terminal state.

D. Supersession

- A pullback starts.
- A newer same-direction completed breakout supersedes the first arm.
- The new breakout receives a distinct arm ID.
- Events, candidates, stops, and targets never cross between the arms.

E. Opposite breakout

- A completed opposite-direction breakout invalidates the arm.
- No later candle can confirm or consume that arm.

F. Boundaries

- Date boundary expires the arm.
- Contract boundary expires the arm.
- E opening at or after 1:00 p.m. ET creates no candidate.
- No entries occur outside 9:30 a.m.–1:00 p.m. ET.

G. Repeated audits

- Repeated observations do not duplicate transitions.
- Terminal states never regress.
- Conflicting observations are diagnostic-only.
- Canonical lifecycle counts remain stable.

H. Independent trades

Create two independent confirmed arms in one deterministic fixture and prove:

- Different arm IDs.
- Different signal/candidate IDs.
- Each trade freezes its own P candle.
- Each stop is based on that trade’s own P range plus the governed eight-tick stop buffer.
- Each target is independently frozen.
- Trade 2 cannot reuse Trade 1’s stop, target, or management context.

Report:

- Unique arms created

- Arms with level interaction
- Patience-armed arms

Required invariants:

- One canonical record per arm ID.
- At most one authoritative terminal outcome per arm.
- At most one candidate per physical P→E signal.
- At most one candidate consumption per arm unless a separately governed multi-entry rule explicitly exists.
- No candidate from a terminal arm.
- No candidate when E opens at or after 1:00 p.m. ET.
- No cross-arm stop or target reuse.
