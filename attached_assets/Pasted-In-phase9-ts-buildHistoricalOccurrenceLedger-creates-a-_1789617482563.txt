In phase9.ts:
- buildHistoricalOccurrenceLedger creates a strategy-specific direct crossing key.
- Direct occurrences still use kind: "patience".
- upsert() replaces occurrenceId using governedOccurrenceId().
- The patience branch of governedOccurrenceId() excludes strategy and frozen consolidation identity.

Therefore, distinct direct setups sharing contract, direction, entry candle, and observation time can receive the same final occurrenceId despite having different ledger keys.

Adding directConsolidationCrossingIdentity as a field does not fix this if the final occurrence ID ignores it.
