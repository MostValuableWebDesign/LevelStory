AUTHORITATIVE STRATEGY TAXONOMY

Preserve these four independent strategies:

1. PATIENCE_CANDLE_CONTINUATION
2. CONSOLIDATION_BREAKOUT_CONTINUATION
3. ORB_PULLBACK_CONTINUATION
4. EQUIVALENT_CANDLE_REVERSAL

Pullback, consolidation, patience candles, entry, stop, target, and runner are components or actions—not additional strategy identities.

All entries must use this sequence:

1. A completed patience candle P.
2. The immediately following completed candle E.
3. E must break P in the trade direction by the configured confirmation buffer.
4. If E does not confirm P, that pair expires.
5. Continue scanning for a new patience candle P2 and evaluate only its immediate next candle E2.
6. Never use a later candle to confirm an earlier patience candle.

CANONICAL ATTRIBUTION

When the same trade satisfies more than one strategy, use this deterministic precedence:

1. ORB_PULLBACK_CONTINUATION
2. CONSOLIDATION_BREAKOUT_CONTINUATION
3. EQUIVALENT_CANDLE_REVERSAL
4. PATIENCE_CANDLE_CONTINUATION

Do not allow generic Patience continuation to claim a trade that has more specific ORB, consolidation-breakout, or equivalent-reversal evidence.

Secondary matched strategies may be retained as diagnostic metadata, but only one canonical strategy owns the trade.

FIX 1 — EQUIVALENT-CANDLE REVERSAL

Equivalent reversal must not require the reversal direction to agree with the preceding continuation trend.

Qualify it using separately governed reversal evidence:

- A valid equivalent opposing full-body candle pattern.
- Evidence of rejection or reversal at an important level.
- Directional reversal confirmation.
- A valid patience candle after the reversal evidence.
- Immediate next-candle confirmation of that patience candle.
- Risk approval.

A reversal may oppose the established 15-minute trend. Do not route this strategy through bullish-continuation or bearish-continuation trend-agreement gates.

Require the patience/entry sequence for this strategy, but do not let generic Patience continuation take canonical ownership of it.

FIX 2 — ORB FIBONACCI INTERACTION

Do not qualify ORB pullback continuation merely because Fibonacci levels were calculated or frozen.

ORB_PULLBACK_CONTINUATION must require all of:

- Completed ORB/NTZ construction.
- A completed breakout beyond ORB/NTZ.
- A qualifying pullback after the breakout.
- Actual interaction with at least one causal qualifying level.
- A completed patience candle.
- Immediate next-candle confirmation.
- Risk approval.

A qualifying interaction may be with:

- ORB/NTZ boundary
- VWAP
- 200 EMA
- Premarket high or low
- Previous-day high or low
- Day-before-previous high or low
- Support/resistance
- A valid Fibonacci retracement level

For Fibonacci, require the pullback candle’s high-low range to touch or come within the configured 12-MES-tick tolerance of a specific Fibonacci level. Merely having a frozen Fibonacci object or a nonempty levels array must not pass the rule.

Evaluate dynamic indicators such as VWAP and 200 EMA at the exact causal candle timestamp—not from their final session values.

Do not require strong-breakout volume/body classification for ORB pullback continuation.

FIX 3 — PATIENCE RE-ARMING AND OCCURRENCE RETENTION

Correct the authoritative Phase 5 patience detector so a failed patience pair does not stop the session scan.

Required behavior:

- P1 forms.
- Only E1 may confirm P1.
- If E1 fails, expires, or breaks the wrong side, record the failed P1/E1 occurrence.
- Resume scanning after E1.
- If P2 forms later, only E2 may confirm P2.
- Continue for every completed occurrence in the causal session.
- Retain all detected pullbacks, patience candles, failed confirmations, valid confirmations, invalidations, and qualified entries.
- Do not stop at the first candidate or first failure.
- Do not reuse one entry candle for multiple patience candles.
- Do not read beyond the evaluation cursor.

A successful sequence must retain explicit timestamps and candle identities for:

- Pullback/level-interaction candle L
- Patience candle P
- Entry-confirmation candle E

FIX 4 — CONSOLIDATION DETECTION

Remove the hard-coded 1.5-point maximum consolidation range.

Consolidation must be based on a configurable, deterministic tight-range/stability rule. Prefer an existing governed configuration value. If none exists, add a named configuration field expressed in MES ticks.

Requirements:

- At least a documented minimum number of completed five-minute candles.
- Stable overlapping price action.
- No material range expansion.
- No exact 45–60-minute duration requirement.
- A consolidation may be shorter or longer when its structure qualifies.
- The threshold must not be an unexplained magic number.
- Persist the threshold and detection evidence in the audit record.

CONSOLIDATION_BREAKOUT_CONTINUATION must require:

- Valid tight/stable consolidation.
- Directional breakout from that consolidation.
- Strong continuation evidence after the breakout.
- A valid patience candle.
- Immediate next-candle confirmation.
- Risk approval.

The patience candle may form after the breakout pullback or after valid post-breakout consolidation. Do not require an unrelated ORB pullback.

CONFIRMED TREND

PATIENCE_CANDLE_CONTINUATION must require confirmed causal 15-minute trend evidence, not merely a non-neutral direction value.

Use the same governed trend-structure evidence used elsewhere, including applicable:

- 15-minute directional structure
- VWAP relationship
- 200 EMA relationship and slope
- Completed-candle-only evidence

Store named evidence fields. Do not infer evidence meaning from array position.

INTRA-SWING REVERSAL RULE

Preserve the separate rule that a pullback greater than 50% from a causal intraday swing peak may signal a reversal.

This is reversal evidence only. It does not create an entry by itself.

Any trade based on this reversal evidence must still wait for:

- A new patience candle
- Its immediate next-candle confirmation
- Risk approval
