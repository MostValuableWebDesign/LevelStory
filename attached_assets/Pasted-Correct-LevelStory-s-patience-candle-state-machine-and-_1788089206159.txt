The current chart incorrectly identifies the 10:05 candle as the patience candle associated with a later entry. Although 10:05 may satisfy the patience-candle shape, its immediate next candle did not surpass its high in the bullish direction. CORE DISTINCTION

Preserve two separate concepts:

1. Patience-candle candidate:
   A completed candle that satisfies the directional patience-candle shape.

2. Confirmed patience setup:
   A patience-candle candidate whose immediate next completed five-minute candle confirms it in the trend direction by the configured entry buffer.

An expired candidate may remain in the historical occurrence ledger for diagnostics, but it must not be presented as the patience candle belonging to a qualified trade.

BULLISH PATIENCE SHAPE

For a bullish trend:

- The current completed candidate candle’s high must remain inside or not surpass the previous completed candle’s high.
- The entire candle does not need to be inside the previous candle.
- Evaluate the top/high side because that is the trend direction.
- The candidate must satisfy all other causal trend, pullback, level-interaction, and risk-context requirements.

BEARISH PATIENCE SHAPE

For a bearish trend:

- The current completed candidate candle’s low must remain inside or not surpass the previous completed candle’s low.
- The entire candle does not need to be inside the previous candle.
- Evaluate the bottom/low side because that is the trend direction.
- The candidate must satisfy all other causal trend, pullback, level-interaction, and risk-context requirements.

IMMEDIATE CONFIRMATION

For candidate P at interval N, only the completed candle at interval N+1 may be E.

Bullish:

- E must trade above P high by the configured confirmation buffer.
- Use the governed MES confirmation buffer, currently 3–4 ticks or the existing configured value.
- Entry occurs at the buffered confirmation price, not automatically at E open.

Bearish:

- E must trade below P low by the configured confirmation buffer.
- Entry occurs at the buffered confirmation price, not automatically at E open.

If the immediate next candle does not confirm P:

- Mark P as `EXPIRED_NO_IMMEDIATE_CONFIRMATION`.
- Do not pair P with any later candle.
- Do not display P as the confirmed patience candle for a trade.
- Do not allow a later candle to become E for that P.
- Resume scanning for a new patience candidate only after the failed immediate confirmation candle.
- When P2 is found, only the candle immediately following P2 may confirm it.
- Continue this P candidate → immediate E evaluation cycle until a confirmed pair is found or the causal window ends.

DEFENSIVE INVARIANTS

A qualified entry must satisfy all of these:

- P is a valid directional patience candidate.
- E is the immediate completed five-minute candle after P.
- E exceeds the directional P boundary by the configured buffer.
- P and E belong to the same contract and New York trading date.
- Neither candle crosses a session or contract boundary.
- Both candles were visible at the causal evaluation cursor.
- The trade references the same confirmed occurrence ID.

Reject or flag any audit record where:

- E is later than the immediate next interval.
- The displayed P is not immediately before E.
- An expired candidate is linked to a later entry.
- The entry candle exists without a confirmed P immediately before it.
