SHARED DEFINITIONS

MES tick size:

0.25 points

MES tick value:

$1.25 per contract

Calculate structural R from the existing modeled entry and revised initial stop:

InitialRiskPoints =
  abs(ModeledEntryPrice − InitialStopPrice)

InitialRiskTicks =
  InitialRiskPoints ÷ 0.25

1R equals the complete price distance between the modeled entry and frozen initial stop.

Use R only for target and management calculations.

Do not use:

- Account balance
- Account percentage
- Dollar-risk budget
- Compounding
- Account value to select contract quantity
- Account value to move the stop

Dollar risk and P/L are outputs only.

ENHANCEMENT 1 — PROFIT TAKING
==================================================

Replace the current fixed 8-tick or 30-tick key-level target buffer with an adaptive 1–2 tick buffer.

At candidate creation, calculate:

ATR_ticks =
  ATR(14) from causally available completed 5-minute candles ÷ 0.25

TargetBufferTicks =
  clamp(
    ceil(ATR_ticks × 0.05),
    minimum 1 tick,
    maximum 2 ticks
  )

Freeze ATR and TargetBufferTicks with the candidate.

Eligible directional key levels:

- VWAP
- 200 EMA
- ORB/NTZ boundaries
- Premarket high/low
- Previous-session high/low
- Two-sessions-ago high/low
- Major support/resistance
- Dynamite/confluence levels

Do not use these as targets:

- Fibonacci levels
- Stops
- Entry buffers
- Runner levels
- Previous-close aliases
- Management annotations
- Non-authoritative or future levels

Target calculation:

Long:

TargetPrice =
  near-side boundary of the selected level
  minus TargetBufferTicks × 0.25

Short:

TargetPrice =
  near-side boundary of the selected level
  plus TargetBufferTicks × 0.25

Target rules:

- Freeze eligible levels using only causally available completed-candle evidence.
- Use the nearest profitable directional level.
- The adjusted target must remain strictly profitable.
- Do not select or change a target using future candles.
- Consider key-level targets up to 1.5R from entry.
- Retain skipped levels and exact rejection reasons in the audit.

Calculate:

TargetR =
  abs(TargetPrice − EntryPrice) ÷ InitialRiskPoints

One-contract minimum:

- The key-level target must offer at least 0.75R.

Two-contract first-target minimum:

- The key-level target must offer at least 0.50R.

If a major opposing level exists before the required minimum reward:

- Reject the trade as `INSUFFICIENT_REWARD_TO_RISK`.
- Do not silently ignore the level.
- Record the obstructing level, adjusted target, TargetR, and required minimum.

If no eligible forward key-level target exists and no major opposing level blocks the trade:

- Use 1R as the fallback target.

ENHANCEMENT 2 — STOP LOSS
==================================================

Replace the fixed 12-tick patience stop buffer with an adaptive 4–8 tick buffer.

Calculate at candidate creation:

StopBufferTicks =
  clamp(
    ceil(ATR_ticks × 0.10),
    minimum 4 ticks,
    maximum 8 ticks
  )

Long stop:

InitialStopPrice =
  patience-candle low − StopBufferTicks × 0.25

Short stop:

InitialStopPrice =
  patience-candle high + StopBufferTicks × 0.25

Stop requirements:

- Calculate the buffer from completed-candle ATR.
- Freeze ATR, StopBufferTicks, patience extreme, and stop price with the candidate.
- The stop must be on the correct side of entry.
- Align the stop to the MES tick grid.
- Do not move the initial stop closer merely to make a trade qualify.
- Stale or legacy stops cannot override the candidate-owned stop.
- Catastrophe protection remains separate and takes precedence.
- Initial protective stops remain intrabar-touch rules; a candle close is not required.

Add a market-structure guard that does not depend on account value:

MaximumRiskTicks =
  clamp(
    ceil(ATR_ticks × 1.50),
    minimum 20 ticks,
    maximum 40 ticks
  )

If:

InitialRiskTicks > MaximumRiskTicks

Then:

- Reject the candidate as `STOP_DISTANCE_TOO_WIDE`.
- Do not reduce contract quantity.
- Do not pull the stop closer.
- Display actual risk ticks, ATR ticks, and the permitted maximum.

This is a volatility and trade-geometry rule—not account-based risk sizing.

ENHANCEMENT 3 — BREAKEVEN AND RECOVERY
==================================================

Replace the unconditional nine-candle breakeven rule with progress-based management after six completed post-entry candles.

Before the first target is reached:

1. Count six completed 5-minute candles after entry.
2. Calculate maximum favorable excursion in R.
3. Evaluate the sixth candle’s completed close.

Favorable close:

- Long: close strictly above entry
- Short: close strictly below entry
- A close exactly at entry is neutral

Breakeven branch:

If:

- Maximum favorable excursion reached at least 0.50R
- And the sixth candle closes favorably

Then:

- Arm the stop at the entry price.
- Make the breakeven stop effective on the next candle.
- Do not apply it retroactively to the sixth candle.
- Keep the original stop active until the breakeven stop becomes effective.
- Record `BREAKEVEN_ARMED` and its effective timestamp.

Recovery branch:

If:

- Maximum favorable excursion did not reach 0.50R
- And the sixth candle closes adversely or neutrally

Then:

- Enter recovery-exit mode.
- Keep the original strategy stop active.
- Do not move the stop to breakeven.
- Beginning with the next candle, exit at entry if price recovers to the entry price.
- Record the exit as `ENTRY_PRICE_RECOVERY_EXIT`.

If price never recovers:

- Keep the original stop active.
- Preserve session-close management.
- Do not fabricate a breakeven exit.

After the first target on a two-contract position:

- Wait for the next completed favorable candle.
- Then move the runner stop to entry if doing so improves protection.
- Do not move the runner to breakeven on the same candle that touches the first target.

Ambiguity:

If the original stop and recovery price are both touched in one historical OHLCV candle and intrabar order is unavailable:

- Apply the adverse stop first.
- Mark the sequence ambiguous.
- Do not score it as an ordinary recovery exit.

ENHANCEMENT 4 — CONTRACT MANAGEMENT
==================================================

Support exactly two fixed-sizing modes:

- Fixed size: 1 contract
- Fixed size: 2 contracts

Default:

1 contract

Contract quantity must not be calculated from:

- Starting account balance
- Account percentage
- Previous P/L
- Dollar-risk budget
- Compounding
- Stop distance

Do not automatically increase or reduce the selected fixed quantity.

ONE CONTRACT

When fixed size is 1:

- Enter one modeled contract.
- If a qualifying key-level target offers at least 0.75R, exit the entire position there.
- Otherwise, if no opposing level blocks the trade, exit the entire position at 1R.
- Do not create a runner.
- Do not apply runner management after the full exit.
- Record one entry leg and one exit leg.

TWO CONTRACTS

When fixed size is 2:

- Enter two modeled contracts at the same candidate-owned entry price.
- If a qualifying key-level target offers at least 0.50R, exit contract 1 there.
- Otherwise, if no opposing level blocks the trade, exit contract 1 at 1R.
- After contract 1 exits, contract 2 becomes the runner.
- The first scale-out may happen only once.
- Record the scale-out and runner as separate execution legs.
- Combine both legs into the candidate’s total P/L.

RUNNER MANAGEMENT

After the first contract exits, calculate:

RunnerBufferTicks =
  clamp(
    ceil(ATR_ticks × 0.10),
    minimum 4 ticks,
    maximum 8 ticks
  )

Long runner:

- Trail below the latest confirmed completed-candle swing low.

Short runner:

- Trail above the latest confirmed completed-candle swing high.

Runner rules:

- Use only completed 5-minute swing evidence.
- The new stop must improve protection.
- Never widen the runner stop.
- Never move a long stop lower.
- Never move a short stop higher.
- Preserve catastrophe-stop and session-close behavior.
- Record every trailing-stop revision and its source candle.
- When multiple valid protective stops exist, use the most protective one that does not violate causal ordering.

P/L CALCULATION

Calculate P/L after modeling execution:

GrossPnl =
  PriceMovementTicks × $1.25 × exited contracts

NetPnl =
  GrossPnl − fees − slippage

For two contracts:

TotalTradeNetPnl =
  contract 1 net P/L + runner net P/L

Starting account balance is used only for displaying combined account results. It must not change contract quantity or management behavior.
