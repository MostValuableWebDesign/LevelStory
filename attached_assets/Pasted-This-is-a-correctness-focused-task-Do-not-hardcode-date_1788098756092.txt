Preserve the improvements already present

Do not remove or regress:

The existing changes are not a complete eligibility lifecycle. Implement the remaining requirements below.

1. Replace stale latestEligibilityBefore behavior

The current Phase 5 implementation still assigns:

latestEligibilityBefore(eligibility, candle.openTime)

to every later patience-shaped candle. This can let one old L interaction qualify P→E pairs much later in the session.

Replace this with a causal eligibility-arm lifecycle.

Each arm must contain:

- Trading date
- Contract symbol
- Strategy candidate
- Direction
- Qualifying level identifier
- Causal level value
- L candle open and close times
- Pullback event ID
- 12-tick tolerance
- Formula version/hash
- Source fingerprint
- State: active, consumed, invalidated, or superseded
- State-change timestamp and reason

An arm becomes active only when a genuine qualifying level interaction L occurs.

A patience candidate may use the arm only when:

- P occurs after L.
- P is completed.
- P has the correct bullish or bearish patience shape.
- P or its immediately preceding comparison candle remains within 12 MES ticks—3.00 points—of the same qualifying level.
- Dynamic levels such as VWAP and EMA 200 use their causal value at L/P, never the session-final value.
- The strategy direction and structural context remain valid.
- The arm has not been consumed, invalidated, or superseded.

Do not use an arbitrary hidden time limit. If an age limit is introduced, it must be governed, configurable, formula-hashed, visible in diagnostics, and tested.

2. Correct P1 → E1 and P2 → E2 behavior

- P1 may only be confirmed by immediate completed E1.
- E1 must exceed P1 in the intended direction by the configured 3–4-tick buffer.
- If E1 fails, P1 expires permanently.
- No later candle may confirm P1.
- Continue scanning for P2 only while the same eligibility arm remains structurally valid.
- P2 may only be confirmed by immediate E2.
- Preserve P1/E1 and P2/E2 as separate immutable occurrence records.
- If context invalidates before P2, require a fresh L.

3. Consume the arm at signal confirmation

Consume the eligibility arm immediately when P→E becomes `ENTRY_CONFIRMED`.

Do not wait for:

- A modeled fill
- A modeled exit
- A completed trade
- Quote data

The existing `executedEntryKeys` protection is execution-level deduplication and is not sufficient. It is currently added only after a modeled trade succeeds.

Separate:

- Signal/eligibility consumption
- Risk decision
- Execution modeling
- Trade creation

Once an arm produces a confirmed P→E signal, later patience-shaped candles cannot reuse that L—even when execution modeling is unavailable or no modeled exit is produced.

A new trade signal requires a fresh qualifying L interaction.

4. Correct signal, risk, and execution outcomes

Do not classify every confirmed signal without a trade as `RISK_REJECTED`.

Use explicit outcomes:

- `ENTRY_CONFIRMATION_FAILED`
- `ENTRY_CONFIRMED`
- `RISK_REJECTED`
- `RISK_APPROVED_EXECUTION_UNAVAILABLE`
- `MODELED_TRADE`

Rules:

- Immediate E failure → `ENTRY_CONFIRMATION_FAILED`
- Valid P→E before risk evaluation → `ENTRY_CONFIRMED`
- Genuine risk-control rejection → `RISK_REJECTED`
- Risk passes but bounded OHLCV execution cannot produce a valid fill/exit pair → `RISK_APPROVED_EXECUTION_UNAVAILABLE`
- Completed modeled fill and exit → `MODELED_TRADE`

`NO_MODELED_EXIT` is an execution-model limitation, not automatically a risk rejection.

6. Use E for confirmed-signal time classification

For confirmed P→E occurrences:

- Primary-window classification must use E’s open time.
- Sorting and selection reasons must use E.
- P’s timestamp may be used only for unconfirmed patience diagnostics.
- Pullback diagnostics use L.
- Modeled trades use their modeled entry/fill observation time.
- The end time is exclusive.

Therefore:

- P from 12:55–1:00 p.m.
- E from 1:00–1:05 p.m.

is outside the primary entry window.
