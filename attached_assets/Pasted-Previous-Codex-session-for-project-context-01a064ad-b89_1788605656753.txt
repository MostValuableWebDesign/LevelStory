- During recovery mode, keep the original stop fully active.

- Exit the entire position at the selected key-level target.

- Do not create a runner.

Two contracts:

- Exit exactly one contract at the selected key-level target.
- If no eligible key level exists, exit exactly one contract at 1R.
- Manage the second contract as a runner.
- After the first contract exits, wait for the next completed favorable candle before arming runner breakeven.
- Do not arm runner breakeven on the target-hit candle.
- Trail the runner using confirmed completed five-minute swings.

- Short runner stop: confirmed swing high + RunnerBufferTicks
- A trailing stop may tighten but must never widen.
