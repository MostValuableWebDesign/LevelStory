Correct the authoritative patience-candle stop-loss calculation in LevelStory.

IMPORTANT RULE

A patience-candle trade must never use the exact patience-candle high or low as its strategy stop. The trade requires an 8-MES-tick breathing range beyond the patience candle.

MES tick size:

- 1 tick = 0.25 index points
- 8 ticks = 2.00 index points

Authoritative stop calculation

For a long trade:

strategyStop = patienceCandle.low - (8 × 0.25)

For a short trade:

strategyStop = patienceCandle.high + (8 × 0.25)

Do not stop the trade merely because price revisits or crosses the patience-candle low/high. A losing exit occurs only when a completed post-entry candle reaches the buffered strategy-stop price.
