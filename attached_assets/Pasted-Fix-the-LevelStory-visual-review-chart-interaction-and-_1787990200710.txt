PART 1 — CORRECT PATIENCE-CANDLE ENTRY RULE

The entry candle must be the immediate next completed five-minute candle following the patience candle.

Canonical sequence:

[P · Patience candle]
→
[E · Immediate-next entry candle]

Do not create a separate trigger candle between P and E.

The entry candle and trigger candle are the same immediate-next five-minute candle.

1. Patience candle completes first

A trade cannot qualify without a valid completed patience candle.

Bullish patience setup:
- active trend must be bullish
- the patience candle’s trend-side high remains within the previous candle’s high under the existing formula definition

Bearish patience setup:
- active trend must be bearish
- the patience candle’s trend-side low remains within the previous candle’s low under the existing formula definition

Preserve the existing finalized patience-candle definition everywhere else.

2. Immediate-next candle only

After the patience candle closes:

- only the immediate next scheduled five-minute candle is eligible for entry
- a later candle cannot trigger the expired patience setup
- if the immediate-next candle is missing from the historical data, the setup cannot use a later candle
- mark the setup as unresolved or rejected because the immediate-next candle was unavailable

Required timestamp relationship:

`entryCandleOpenTime === patienceCandleCloseTime`

For normal completed five-minute candles:

`entryCandleOpenTime === patienceCandleOpenTime + 5 minutes`

3. Buffered entry

Bullish:
- the immediate-next candle must reach 3–4 MES ticks above the patience candle’s high
- place the long entry at the configured buffered price

Bearish:
- the immediate-next candle must reach 3–4 MES ticks below the patience candle’s low
- place the short entry at the configured buffered price

MES tick size:
- 0.25 points
- 3 ticks = 0.75 points
- 4 ticks = 1.00 point

Use the configured 3- or 4-tick setting. Do not introduce a new arbitrary buffer.

4. Expiration

If the immediate-next five-minute candle does not reach the buffered entry price:

- no entry occurs
- the patience setup expires
- no later candle can trigger that patience setup
- a new entry requires a new valid patience candle

5. Visual event markers

Use:

- `P` or the relevant numbered marker on the patience candle
- `E` or the relevant numbered marker on the immediate-next entry candle only when entry actually occurred

Do not display a separate `T` candle between P and E.

If the immediate-next candle fails:

- show the patience event
- do not show an entry marker
- show in the event index:
  `No entry · Immediate-next candle did not reach the buffered price`

If the immediate-next candle is missing:

- show:
  `No entry · Immediate-next five-minute candle unavailable`

If no valid patience candle exists:

- no entry marker may exist
- the trade must not qualify

6. Entry audit requirements

Every entry must record:

- patience candle open time
- patience candle close time
- entry candle open time
- entry candle close time
- patience candle high
- patience candle low
- direction
- buffer ticks
- buffered entry trigger price
- modeled fill price
- modeled fill observation time
- patience audit ID
- associated trade ID

Reject any trade whose audit cannot prove that its entry candle was the immediate-next five-minute candle.

7. Patience/entry tests

Add deterministic tests proving:

- no entry can exist without a valid patience candle
- the entry candle is exactly the immediate next five-minute candle
- bullish entry requires the configured buffer above the patience high
- bearish entry requires the configured buffer below the patience low
- a later candle cannot trigger an expired patience setup
- a missing immediate-next interval cannot be skipped
- a new patience candle can create a new independent setup
- P and E chart markers use the exact audited candles
- no separate trigger candle is inserted between P and E
- visual-review trade association uses the exact patience and entry timestamps
