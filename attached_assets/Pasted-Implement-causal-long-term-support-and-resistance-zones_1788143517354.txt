Implement causal long-term support and resistance zones as a separate, cached analysis layer.

This is the compute-heavy part. Do not run a full historical replay during implementation.

Data and timeframe:

- Use completed one-hour MES bars.
- Build rolling six-month and one-year lookbacks using trading sessions.
- At every historical evaluation cursor, use only bars completed before that cursor.
- Never calculate historical zones using later candles.

Detection:

1. Identify confirmed one-hour swing highs and swing lows.
2. A resistance touch is a confirmed rejection from a swing-high area.
3. A support touch is a confirmed bounce from a swing-low area.
4. Cluster nearby pivots into zones using a governed width.
5. Start with a configurable default zone width of 12 MES ticks, not a hardcoded constant.
6. Require at least three independent touches.
7. Require touches on at least three separate trading dates.
8. Prevent several adjacent candles from counting as several independent touches.
9. Preserve role changes: a former resistance zone may later act as support, but store both histories.
10. Never infer a zone from visual proximity alone.

Each zone must store:

- Zone ID.
- Lower and upper price.
- Midpoint.
- Support, resistance, or role-flip classification.
- Six-month or one-year lookback.
- Touch count.
- Independent trading dates.
- First and latest qualifying timestamps.
- Source pivot timestamps.
- Strength grade based on verified evidence.
- Formula/configuration hash.

Chart behavior:

- Render zones as shaded horizontal bands, not single hard-price lines.
- Support zones: black/gray band with a support label.
- Resistance zones: black band with a resistance label.
- Show midpoint and zone boundaries in the inspector.
- Allow the zone to qualify a pullback when the candle range comes within the governed 12-tick pullback tolerance.
- Do not overcrowd the chart: show the strongest nearby zones by default and place the rest under “Additional levels.”

Performance:

- Cache zones by source fingerprint, contract schedule, lookback, timeframe, detector version, and configuration hash.
- Recalculate only when those inputs change.
- Do not scan one-minute data.
- Do not rebuild the market-data index.

Testing:

- Three independent resistance rejections form a zone.
- Three independent support bounces form a zone.
- Adjacent candles count as one interaction.
- Fewer than three dates do not form a zone.
- No future access.
- Six-month and one-year windows expire old evidence correctly.
- Role-flip evidence is preserved.
- Cache identity changes when detector settings change.

Run focused zone-detector tests, chart tests, typechecks, and git diff --check only.

Do not activate these zones in trade qualification until their chart output is visually reviewed and approved.
