1. PATIENCE_CANDLE_CONTINUATION
--------------------------------------------------

Current problem:

The strategy currently treats any non-neutral trend direction as a confirmed trend. That is insufficient.

Required context:

A. Confirmed causal 15-minute directional trend.
B. A qualifying continuation context, normally a pullback to a machine-visible level.
C. A trend-aligned patience candle P.
D. Immediate next-candle confirmation E.
E. Risk approval.

CONFIRMED 15-MINUTE TREND

Create or reuse one shared function:

hasConfirmedDirectionalTrend(trendEvidence, direction)

It must use the same causal 15-minute trend-structure evidence required by the other directional continuation strategies.

Do not pass merely because:

trend.direction !== neutral

Require the existing confirmed trend evidence, including applicable:

- Completed 15-minute structure
- Higher-high/higher-low or lower-high/lower-low evidence
- 15-minute directional slope evidence
- Required completed-candle lookback
- Absence of neutral, unclear, or conflicting trend state
- Machine-visible causal timestamps

Long patience continuation requires confirmed bullish evidence.

Short patience continuation requires confirmed bearish evidence.

If direction is present but not confirmed, record:

TREND_DIRECTION_PRESENT_BUT_UNCONFIRMED

and reject the strategy without deleting any detected pullback or patience evidence.

PATIENCE RE-ARMING

For every P:

- Evaluate only its immediate next completed five-minute candle E.
- If E confirms by the configured 3–4 tick buffer, continue to risk approval.
- If E fails, expire that P only.
- Keep the valid underlying strategy context active.
- Continue scanning for another P.
- E may become the next P when it independently satisfies the patience definition.
- A later candle may never confirm an older P.

--------------------------------------------------
2. STRONG_BREAKOUT_AFTER_CONSOLIDATION
--------------------------------------------------

Current problem:

The detector hardcodes exactly 9–12 five-minute candles and a 45–60 minute consolidation. Duration should not define the edge.

Required sequence:

A. Tight/stable consolidation range detected.
B. No material range expansion during the consolidation.
C. Directional breakout from the frozen range.
D. Strong breakout evidence specific to this strategy.
E. Continuation after the breakout.
F. A valid post-breakout patience candle P.
G. Immediate E confirmation.
H. Risk approval.

CONSOLIDATION DETECTION

Detect consolidation primarily from price behavior:

- Stable bounded high-low range
- Repeated completed candles remaining inside or near the frozen range
- No material progressive range expansion
- No persistent directional displacement before breakout
- Range width acceptable relative to an existing configurable tick/ATR measure
- Causal start and end timestamps
- Frozen range high and low before breakout

Candle count and elapsed time may be:

- Diagnostic evidence
- A configurable minimum data-quality requirement

They must not require exactly:

- 9–12 candles
- 45–60 minutes

Do not impose a maximum consolidation duration unless a separately governed configuration explicitly defines one.

Remove “near the NTZ” as a universal hard requirement. NTZ proximity may be recorded as confluence, but consolidation can occur at another machine-visible area.

STRONG BREAKOUT

Only this strategy should require the strong-breakout gates, including the governed combination of:

- Directional close outside the frozen consolidation range
- Meaningful displacement
- Supporting volume
- Candle-body/close-location evidence
- Continued movement or completed-candle continuation

A strong breakout alone is not an entry.

Entry must still wait for a post-breakout P and its immediate E confirmation.

3. ORB_BREAK_PULLBACK_CONTINUATION
--------------------------------------------------

Current problem:

ORB continuation is being routed through strong-breakout gates that belong only to STRONG_BREAKOUT_AFTER_CONSOLIDATION.

Required sequence:

A. Finalized ORB/NTZ.
B. Completed candle breaks and closes outside the ORB/NTZ.
C. Direction agrees with the required confirmed directional evidence.
D. A qualifying pullback occurs.
E. Pullback interacts with either:
   - A structural/dynamic qualifying level within the shared 12-tick area; or
   - A valid causal frozen Fibonacci level after a valid swing exists.
F. A directionally aligned patience candle P forms.
G. Immediate E confirms P using the 3–4 tick entry buffer.
H. Risk approval passes.

ORB breakout requirements:

- Finalized opening range
- Completed close outside the range
- Valid direction
- No future access

ORB continuation must not require:

- Strong breakout classification
- Strong breakout body ratio
- Strong breakout volume classification
- Consolidation-breakout classification

Those may be recorded as optional confluence but cannot block an otherwise valid ORB pullback continuation.

The required directional continuation after the pullback is supplied by the valid P/E confirmation sequence. Do not require a separate strong-breakout gate before allowing the pullback to be evaluated.

LEVEL INTERACTION

Use the authoritative shared pullback rule:

- 12 MES ticks = 3.00 points by default
- Full L-candle high-low range
- VWAP at L
- EMA 200 at L
- Fixed structural level value visible at L
- Shared detector/Visual Review qualification helper
- ATR diagnostic only

Fibonacci is optional alternative evidence, not universally mandatory.

Do not create Fibonacci levels until a valid causal swing and qualifying pullback context exist.

SHARED PATIENCE RULE
--------------------------------------------------

Apply the existing directional patience definition:

Bullish P:

- Confirmed bullish strategy direction
- P’s high does not exceed the preceding completed candle’s high
- The whole candle does not need to be inside the preceding candle

Bearish P:

- Confirmed bearish strategy direction
- P’s low does not exceed the preceding completed candle’s low downward
- The whole candle does not need to be inside the preceding candle

Each P can be confirmed only by its immediate next completed five-minute candle.

Long E:

- Must exceed P high by the configured 3–4 tick buffer

Short E:

- Must exceed P low downward by the configured 3–4 tick buffer

If E fails:

- Preserve P/E attempt
- Expire P only
- Continue searching while the strategy context remains valid
