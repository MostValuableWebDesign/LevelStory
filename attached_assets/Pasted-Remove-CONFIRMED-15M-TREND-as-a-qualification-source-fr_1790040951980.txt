PROBLEM

The generic trend calculation requires eight completed 15-minute candles. Starting from the regular-session open, that can delay direction confirmation until approximately 11:30 a.m. ET and prevent otherwise valid early trade candidates from appearing.

Verified current paths still include:

- `PatienceDirectionSource` contains `CONFIRMED_15M_TREND`.
- The main market-data flow chooses:
  `ORB trend → breakout direction → CONFIRMED_15M_TREND`.
- `patienceCandleEngine()` defaults to `CONFIRMED_15M_TREND`.
- `phase5PatienceAnalysis()` defaults to `CONFIRMED_15M_TREND`.
- `evaluatePatienceCandleContinuation()` can require the full confirmed 15-minute trend again.
- Uploaded-chart analysis explicitly labels its supplied direction as `CONFIRMED_15M_TREND`.
- Phase 9 diagnostics count patience shapes sourced from `CONFIRMED_15M_TREND`.

REQUESTED STRATEGY POLICY

Only `PEAK_RETRACEMENT_REVERSAL` may require the generic confirmed 15-minute trend.

Use earlier strategy-native causal direction for continuation strategies:

1. Active confirmed causal ORB trend.
2. Otherwise, a current valid non-failed ORB breakout direction.
3. Otherwise, no executable continuation direction exists.

Do not fall back to the generic 15-minute trend.

A bullish or bearish generic 15-minute trend may remain visible as diagnostic context. It must not create, suppress, redirect, or invalidate a non-Peak candidate.

AUTHORITATIVE STRATEGY BEHAVIOR

### 1. Peak Retracement Reversal

Preserve the confirmed 15-minute trend requirement.

It must still require:

- eight completed 15-minute candles;
- bullish or bearish direction;
- score of at least `+5` or at most `-5`;
- matching structure;
- matching VWAP evidence;
- matching EMA evidence;
- matching EMA-slope evidence;
- proposed reversal direction opposite that confirmed trend.

A neutral, incomplete, mixed, or unconfirmed trend must fail Peak Retracement Reversal.

Prefer calling the canonical `hasConfirmedDirectionalTrend()` helper explicitly in the Peak evaluator so this exception cannot be weakened accidentally.

### 2. Patience Candle Continuation

3. ORB Pullback Continuation
Remove the generic 15-minute trend fallback completely.
Require an active causal ORB trend or current valid non-failed breakout direction. If neither exists, fail closed with no trade candidate.

- the latest candle;
- VWAP alone;
- EMA alone;
- a partially formed 15-minute candle;
- a later candle that was unavailable when P formed;
- a failed or stale breakout;
- a future ORB-trend transition.
4. Early ORB Momentum Continuation

- generic 15-minute trend;

5. Strong Breakout After Consolidation

Do not add a generic trend gate.
Do not alter its frozen consolidation identity, midpoint stop, catastrophe stop, entry boundary, replay chronology, or execution rules.
6. Equivalent Candle Reversal
Do not add a generic confirmed-trend requirement.
Preserve its pattern-owned reversal direction and immediate-trigger rules. Generic trend may remain diagnostic context used to interpret a pattern, but it must not become a mandatory qualification gate.
CAUSAL TIMING RULES
The chosen direction must have existed when the candidate became eligible.
For an ORB-trend source:
- the ORB transition must already be effective at or before P opens;
- preserve the exact ORB epoch ID;
- a later trend transition cannot authorize an earlier P;
- a reversal of the ORB trend must continue to invalidate or supersede the appropriate pending arm under existing rules.
For a breakout-direction source:
- the breakout must already be detected;
- it must not be failed, invalidated, superseded, or stale;
- its timestamp must be no later than the P eligibility boundary;
- preserve the source breakout identity and direction in the occurrence and candidate.
If both sources are absent, return no executable direction and create no candidate or modeled trade.
If an active causal ORB trend and breakout evidence conflict, follow the repository’s canonical ORB-epoch policy. Do not silently combine incompatible evidence. Fail closed if the evidence cannot be reconciled causally.
