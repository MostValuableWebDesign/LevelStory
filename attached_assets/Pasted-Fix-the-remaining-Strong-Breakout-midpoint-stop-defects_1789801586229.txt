into `CONSOLIDATION_BREAKOUT_CONTINUATION`.

Later, `isStrongBreakoutOccurrence()` treats the generic canonical identity as Strong Breakout. An Extended NTZ occurrence can therefore receive the midpoint stop after its original identity has been erased.

Required behavior:

- Preserve the exact detected strategy identity separately from its canonical strategy family.
- Add an authoritative field such as `specificStrategyId`, `detectedStrategyId`, or an equivalent existing field.
- Carry it through:
  - Audit
  - Occurrence
  - Causal identity
  - Candidate
  - Management context
  - Execution
  - Trade
  - Batch aggregation
  - Visual Review
- `isStrongBreakoutOccurrence()` must require affirmative evidence that the specific strategy is `STRONG_BREAKOUT_AFTER_CONSOLIDATION`.
- A generic `CONSOLIDATION_BREAKOUT_CONTINUATION` value alone must not be enough to authorize the midpoint stop when the original specific identity is unavailable.
- Fail closed when a direct consolidation occurrence cannot distinguish Strong Breakout from Extended NTZ.
- Do not infer the specific strategy from candle geometry.

Preserve `EXTENDED_NTZ_CONSOLIDATION_BREAKOUT` behavior. It must not receive:

- The midpoint stop

- `CONSOLIDATION_MIDPOINT_REENTRY_STOP`

3. FIX FALSE STOP-HIT AND ACTIVATION TIMESTAMPS

Current defect:

`stopHitTimestamp` is populated whenever the midpoint exit reason was configured, even if the actual exit was:

- Target
- Runner
- Session close
- Breakeven
- Manual/open

A reproduced target-winning trade currently reports a midpoint `stopHitTimestamp`.

Required behavior:

Set `consolidationMidpointStop.stopHitTimestamp` only when:

`exitReason === "CONSOLIDATION_MIDPOINT_REENTRY_STOP"`

For every other outcome, it must be `null`.

Timestamp precision:

- With complete ordered intrabar evidence, use the exact ordered stop-hit timestamp.
- If a gap is known at candle open, use the candle-open timestamp.
- With OHLC-only evidence that proves the stop was touched but cannot establish an exact intrabar time, leave the exact timestamp null.
- Preserve the exit candle’s open and close timestamps separately as bounded evidence.
- Never substitute candle-close time as the exact stop-hit time.
- Never stamp a target or session-close timestamp as a stop hit.

Apply the same standard to activation:

- Exact ordered fill: use the ordered fill timestamp.
- Entry known at candle open: use the candle-open timestamp.
- Modeled intrabar entry without ordered evidence: exact activation timestamp is null.
- Do not use candle-close time as a fabricated fill or activation time.

Ensure Visual Review distinguishes:

- Exact timestamp
- Candle-bounded but exact time unknown
- Stop never hit

Required behavior:

  - Raw midpoint
  - Tick-aligned stop
  - Distance from midpoint to trigger in ticks
  - Direction
  - Calculation version
- Midpoint penetration distance may be fractional when the midpoint falls between valid ticks. Do not round it into a misleading integer.
- If `stopBufferTicks` is structurally required, make it nullable or explicitly not applicable for Strong Breakout.
- Candidate, management context, audit, execution, trade, and Visual Review must report identical values.
- Remove or update tests that assert `stopBufferTicks === 8` for Strong Breakout.
- Do not change the governed eight-tick entry threshold unless separately required by the existing strategy. Entry-buffer evidence and stop-buffer evidence must remain distinct.
