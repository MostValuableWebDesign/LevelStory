Add a single-active-trade rule across all LevelStory strategies and Shadow Account Replay.

Core rule

Never enter another trade while any previously entered trade is still active.

This applies regardless of whether the open trade is currently:

- Profitable
- Losing
- At breakeven
- Waiting for its target
- Waiting for its stop
- In recovery mode
- Managing a runner

A new trade may enter only after the existing position is completely closed.

A position is completely closed only when:

- Every contract reaches its profit exit
- Every remaining contract is stopped out
- A breakeven or recovery exit closes the entire position
- A runner exits
- The complete position closes at the session boundary

Two-contract behavior

Taking profit on the first contract does not make the account flat.

If the runner remains open:

- The trade remains active.
- Block every new entry.
- Continue managing the runner normally.
- Permit another entry only after the runner closes.

Multiple-occurrence behavior

Continue detecting and recording every qualifying strategy occurrence.

Do not merge, delete, or hide a confirmed occurrence because another trade is open. Each occurrence must retain:

- Occurrence identity
- P and E candles
- Strategy attribution
- Entry trigger
- Stop and target plan
- Qualification evidence

However, distinguish between:

- Qualified strategy candidate
- Account-entered trade
- Candidate blocked by an existing active position

A qualified candidate occurring while another trade is active must not become an entered trade or contribute P/L. Record it with:

ACCOUNT_ENTRY_BLOCKED_ACTIVE_POSITION

The explanation must identify:

- Blocking trade ID
- Blocking candidate ID
- Blocking trade entry time
- Blocking trade expected/current status
- New candidate ID
- New candidate entry time
- Whether the blocking position had one contract or a remaining runner

Chronological processing

Process all candidates across all strategies and selected dates in strict chronological order.

For every candidate:

1. Determine its modeled entry timestamp.
2. Check the account position state at that timestamp.
3. If the account is flat, enter the candidate.
4. If a position remains active, block the candidate.
5. Continue managing the active position candle by candle.
6. Return the account to flat only after all contracts close.
7. Permit the next chronologically eligible candidate after that closure.

Do not inspect a future exit to retroactively approve an earlier candidate.

Same-timestamp handling

If an existing position and a new candidate share the same timestamp:

- Permit the new trade only when the prior trade has an authoritative full-exit event effective at or before that timestamp.
- If event ordering cannot be determined from OHLCV data, apply the conservative rule and block the new entry.
- Record the ambiguity reason.

Scope

Apply the single-position restriction account-wide across:

- Every strategy
- Long and short trades
- Early ORB Momentum Continuation
- Pullback and consolidation strategies
- Uploaded-chart candidates
- Historical Visual Review
- Combined multi-date replay
- One- and two-contract modes

Do not allow a new opposite-direction trade to reverse the position automatically.

Visual Review and reporting

Display all qualified candidates, including blocked candidates.

For each candidate show:

- Qualified: yes/no
- Account entry: entered/blocked
- Blocking reason
- Blocking trade
- Position state at candidate time
- Full-exit timestamp that returned the account to flat

Performance metrics must distinguish:

- Qualified candidates
- Entered trades
- Active-position-blocked candidates
- Finalized trades
- Open trades
- Wins and losses
- Realized P/L

Blocked candidates must not affect:

- Realized P/L
- Win rate
- Average win/loss
- Profit factor
- Expectancy
- Drawdown

Regression tests

Add tests proving:

1. A second candidate is blocked while the first trade is losing but open.
2. A second candidate is blocked while the first trade is profitable but has not exited.
3. A second candidate is blocked while a two-contract runner remains active.
4. A second candidate may enter after the first trade closes at its target.
5. A second candidate may enter after the first trade stops out.
6. A second candidate may enter after a runner fully exits.
7. Opposite-direction candidates cannot reverse an active position.
8. Every blocked occurrence remains visible as a qualified candidate.
9. Blocked candidates do not contribute P/L or performance statistics.
10. Combined multi-date replay carries the position state chronologically.
11. Position state resets correctly after session-close liquidation.
12. Same-timestamp ambiguity applies the conservative blocking rule.
13. Cached and regenerated review sets produce identical position decisions.
14. One- and two-contract accounting remains correct.
15. No strategy can bypass the shared account-position gate.

Use one authoritative account-position gate rather than implementing separate versions inside individual strategies.
