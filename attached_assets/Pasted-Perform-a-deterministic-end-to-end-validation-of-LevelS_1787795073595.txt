Perform a deterministic end-to-end validation of LevelStory’s complete phased strategy engine.

This is a futures Shadow Mode application. Do not add broker integration, paper orders, or live orders. Do not change strategy rules merely to make tests pass.

OBJECTIVE

Prove that the application correctly moves through the complete lifecycle:

market data
→ NTZ/ORB formation
→ breakout-quality evaluation
→ continuation
→ pullback or qualifying consolidation
→ patience candle
→ immediate-next-candle trigger
→ risk approval
→ shadow entry
→ target, stop, or runner exit
→ journal and performance reporting

CREATE DETERMINISTIC REPLAY FIXTURES

Create fixed, named OHLCV fixtures with known timestamps, tick sizes, expected states, and expected results.

Fixture 1 — Weak ORB probe rejected

- First three completed five-minute candles form the 09:30–09:45 ET NTZ/ORB.
- A completed candle closes slightly beyond the ORB.
- Volume and continuation are insufficient.
- Expected:
  - breakout.detected=false;
  - state is ORB_PROBE_WAIT, WEAK_BREAK_WAIT, or WAITING_FOR_CONTINUATION;
  - ORB signal is not confirmed;
  - no pullback evaluation begins;
  - no patience entry is eligible;
  - no risk approval;
  - no shadow position.

Fixture 2 — Bullish A+ continuation setup

- Bullish 15-minute trend.
- Strong close above ORB high with qualifying volume and continuation.
- Pullback reaches or comes within configured proximity of ORB high and at least one critical level, VWAP, EMA, Fibonacci level, or valid confluence.
- Pullback volume is weaker than breakout volume.
- Bullish patience candle high does not exceed the previous completed candle’s high.
- Only the immediate next candle reaches four ticks above the patience high.
- Expected:
  - qualified long breakout;
  - pullback observed;
  - patience valid;
  - long entry triggered at the exact buffered price;
  - stop below patience low;
  - catastrophe stop beyond strategy stop;
  - positive whole-number contract quantity;
  - risk approved;
  - Shadow Mode position recorded.

Fixture 3 — Bearish A+ continuation setup

- Bearish 15-minute trend.
- Strong close below ORB low with qualifying volume and continuation.
- Valid pullback with weaker volume.
- Bearish patience candle low does not go below the previous completed candle’s low.
- Only the immediate next candle reaches four ticks below the patience low.
- Expected:
  - qualified short setup;
  - exact buffered short entry;
  - stop above patience high;
  - catastrophe stop beyond strategy stop;
  - risk approved;
  - Shadow Mode position recorded.

Fixture 4 — Patience candle expires

- Valid breakout, pullback, trend, and patience candle.
- The immediate next candle does not reach the full four-tick entry buffer.
- A later candle reaches it.
- Expected:
  - PATIENCE_CANDLE_EXPIRED;
  - later candle cannot reuse the pattern;
  - no entry;
  - no shadow position.

Fixture 5 — Opposite-side invalidation

- Valid patience candle.
- Immediate next candle crosses the opposite patience extreme before reaching the entry buffer.
- Expected:
  - OPPOSITE_SIDE_INVALIDATION;
  - setup permanently rejected;
  - no later recovery or entry.

Fixture 6 — Same-candle ambiguity

- Immediate next OHLC candle contains both the entry buffer and opposite invalidation price.
- No intrabar sequence data exists.
- Expected:
  - AMBIGUOUS_EVENT_ORDER;
  - setup rejected;
  - do not assume the profitable event occurred first.

Fixture 7 — Risk rejection

- Fully qualified setup and entry trigger.
- Daily loss lockout, excessive spread, stale data, insufficient liquidity, duplicate entry, or invalid sizing blocks the plan.
- Expected:
  - riskPlan.allowed=false;
  - contracts=0;
  - setup riskApproval fails;
  - no shadow position;
  - decision explanation includes the exact risk reason.

Fixture 8 — Profit-target exit

- Qualified Shadow Mode entry.
- Price reaches the selected futures profit target between $50 and $100.
- Calculate the target from contract tick size and dollar value per tick.
- Include slippage and fees.
- Expected:
  - target exit;
  - correct gross and net P&L;
  - completed journal record.

Fixture 9 — Strategy-stop exit

- Qualified entry.
- Price crosses the patience-based strategy stop.
- Expected:
  - stop evaluated according to the configured intrabar/close policy;
  - exit recorded once;
  - correct P&L, fees, and reason;
  - no duplicate exit.

Fixture 10 — Runner exit

- Enter more than one futures contract.
- Take the planned target contracts off at the $50–$100 target.
- Leave at least one runner.
- Freeze the runner’s reference impulse.
- Price retraces adversely by 40% or more from the most favorable excursion.
- Expected:
  - runner exits at the defined threshold;
  - frozen reference does not move backward;
  - target and runner P&L are accounted for separately;
  - journal contains the complete lifecycle.

REQUIRED INVARIANTS

Add assertions proving:

1. Every public decision comes from the phased engine.
2. Breakout, setup, patience, risk plan, and execution share one direction.
3. breakout.detected=false can never produce a confirmed ORB signal.
4. riskPlan.allowed=false can never produce a qualified entry.
5. contracts=0 can never produce a shadow position.
6. Entry cannot occur before a qualifying breakout and pullback/consolidation.
7. Entry cannot occur without a valid patience candle.
8. Only the immediate next candle may trigger.
9. Entry requires the complete configured tick buffer.
10. No setup can create more than one entry or exit.
11. All prices are rounded to the futures contract tick size.
12. Timestamps use the correct America/New_York session boundaries.
13. Journal and dashboard metrics agree with executed Shadow Mode trades.
14. Descriptive evaluations are not counted as trades.
15. No code path can submit an external order.

- Run TypeScript typecheck.
- Run all unit tests.
- Run all integration tests.
- Run all replay/backtest tests.
- Report exact totals: passed, failed, skipped.
- Report the outcome of every fixture.
- Report the exact entry, stop, catastrophe stop, target, contracts, gross P&L, fees, slippage, and net P&L for fixtures that trade.
- Include the final state timeline for each fixture.
- Do not mark this phase complete if:
  - any test fails;
  - any invariant is violated;
  - the replay produces zero qualified entries;
  - bullish or bearish execution remains untested;
  - target, stop, and runner exits are not demonstrated.
