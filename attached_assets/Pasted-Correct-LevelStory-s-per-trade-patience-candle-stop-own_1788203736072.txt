Correct LevelStory’s per-trade patience-candle stop ownership.

Observed defect

Two same-session trades were created:

- Trade 1 used its patience candle and produced a stop at 7471.25.
- Trade 2 entered at 7494.50 but incorrectly reused Trade 1’s 7471.25 stop.
- Trade 2 must calculate a new stop from the patience candle belonging to Trade 2.

Authoritative rule

Every confirmed P→E occurrence owns an independent management plan.

For MES, the stop buffer is 8 ticks = 2.00 points:

Long:
strategyStop = associatedPatienceCandle.low - 2.00

Short:
strategyStop = associatedPatienceCandle.high + 2.00

Do not use 3.00 points. Eight MES ticks equals 2.00 points.

Required correction

1. Bind every candidate, fill, trade, and frozen management context to the same canonical signal occurrence:
   - signalOccurrenceId
   - candidateId
   - P candle open time
   - E candle open time
   - direction
   - contract
   - trading date

2. When creating a trade, resolve its patience candle exclusively through that trade’s signal occurrence. Never use:
   - The first patience candle of the session
   - The most recently cached stop
   - Another trade’s audit record
   - Session-level mutable management state
   - A strategy-level shared stop

3. Freeze a separate management object for each candidate-owned trade containing:
   - patienceCandleOpenTime
   - patienceCandleHigh
   - patienceCandleLow
   - stopBufferTicks: 8
   - tickSize: 0.25
   - derived strategyStop
   - candidateId
   - signalOccurrenceId

4. Key frozen management by candidateId or tradeId, not merely by session, strategy, direction, or contract.

5. A later P→E confirmation must create a new management plan and recompute its stop even when:
   - It occurs on the same date
   - It has the same direction
   - It uses the same strategy
   - It shares some qualifying levels with an earlier trade

6. Do not retroactively modify Trade 1 when Trade 2 is created.

7. Exit replay must use the stop frozen for the exact trade being replayed.

8. Propagate the independently calculated stop consistently through:
   - Candidate record
   - Trade record
   - Frozen management context
   - OHLCV outcome replay
   - Exit record
   - Audit evidence
   - Visual Review stop line
   - Trade P/L and report metrics

Required deterministic regression test

Construct two same-session, same-direction long P→E trades:

Trade 1:
- P1 has a known low
- Expected stop = P1.low - 2.00

Trade 2:
- P2 has a different, higher low
- Expected stop = P2.low - 2.00

Prove:

- Both candidates and trades have different canonical identities.
- Trade 1 uses only P1’s stop.
- Trade 2 uses only P2’s stop.
- Trade 2 does not reuse Trade 1’s stop.
- A candle touching Trade 1’s stop after Trade 2 enters does not stop Trade 2 unless it also reaches Trade 2’s own stop.
- Each Visual Review chart displays the correct trade-specific stop.
- Each exit and P/L calculation uses the appropriate stop.

Add the equivalent short test:

- Trade 1 stop = P1.high + 2.00
- Trade 2 stop = P2.high + 2.00

Also retain the existing boundary tests:

- Seven ticks beyond the associated P extreme survives.
- The eighth tick triggers the stop.
- The E candle is entry evidence only.
- Management begins with the first completed candle after E.
