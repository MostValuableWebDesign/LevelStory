Authoritative trading rules

- MES uses 5-minute execution candles.
- A pullback/level interaction creates an active eligibility arm.
- The qualifying level uses the governed 12-tick MES tolerance: 3.00 points.
- A patience candle P must occur after L while the qualifying level context remains valid.
- Entry E must be the immediately following completed 5-minute candle.
- E must exceed P in the intended direction by the configured 3–4-tick confirmation buffer.
- If E fails, that P expires. Continue looking for a new P, but only while the same pullback context remains genuinely valid.
- Once P→E confirms, consume that eligibility arm. Another entry requires a fresh qualifying pullback/level interaction.
- Primary entry hours are 9:30 a.m.–1:00 p.m. America/New_York.
- Shadow Mode only. Do not add live orders, paper orders, broker integration, fabricated quotes, or fabricated fills.

1. Prevent stale pullback eligibility

The current patience engine applies `latestEligibilityBefore()` to every later patience-shaped candle. This lets one morning L candle qualify multiple P→E pairs throughout the day.

Replace this with an explicit causal eligibility-arm lifecycle.

An eligibility arm must be identified by:

- Trading date
- Contract
- Strategy candidate
- Direction
- Qualifying level identifier
- Qualifying level-interaction candle L
- Level value at the causal timestamp
- Formula hash/version
- Source fingerprint

The arm becomes active at L.

A P candidate may use the arm only when:

- P occurs after L.
- P is completed.
- P has the correct directional patience shape.
- P itself, or its immediately preceding comparison candle, remains within the governed 12-tick zone of the same qualifying level.
- For dynamic indicators such as VWAP or EMA 200, use their causal value at the relevant L/P timestamp—not the session-final value.
- The strategy direction and required context remain valid.
- No confirmed entry has already consumed the arm.
- No invalidating structural event has terminated the arm.

Invalidate the arm when:

- Price materially leaves the qualifying level zone and the pullback/consolidation context is no longer valid.
- An opposing structural breakout or governed reversal invalidates the direction.
- The session or contract changes.
- A confirmed P→E entry consumes it.
- A newer qualifying L supersedes it for the same strategy, direction, and level.

Do not add a hidden arbitrary duration merely to remove afternoon occurrences. If an age limit is necessary, make it governed, configurable, formula-hashed, visible in diagnostics, and supported by tests. Prefer structural and proximity validity.

2. Preserve P1 failure → P2 re-arming correctly

- P1 may only be confirmed by immediate E1.
- If E1 fails, P1 expires permanently.
- E2 can never confirm P1.
- Continue scanning for P2 only if the eligibility arm is still valid.
- P2 may only be confirmed by immediate E2.
- If the level context was invalidated before P2, require a fresh L.
- Do not overwrite an earlier occurrence record.
- Retain expired and confirmed attempts separately in the occurrence ledger.

3. Consume confirmed eligibility

After a confirmed P→E pair:

- Mark the exact eligibility arm consumed.
- Do not produce additional confirmed entries from that same L and level interaction.
- Require a fresh pullback interaction before another trade can qualify.
- Preserve the original confirmed occurrence as immutable evidence.

This must stop one 10:15 L from generating confirmed pairs at 10:35, 10:45, 11:05, noon, and the afternoon without fresh pullbacks.

4. Correct occurrence outcome taxonomy

The historical OHLCV source has no bid/ask quotes, so a valid signal may not receive a modeled fill. Do not call this a risk rejection.

Use separate outcomes:

- `RISK_REJECTED`: risk controls genuinely rejected the setup.
- `RISK_APPROVED_EXECUTION_UNAVAILABLE`: strategy and risk passed, but a modeled fill was unavailable because quote evidence was absent.
- `MODELED_TRADE`: a valid modeled execution exists.
- `ENTRY_CONFIRMATION_FAILED`: immediate E failed.
- `ENTRY_CONFIRMED`: P→E confirmed before execution modeling.

Do not convert every `ENTRY_TRIGGERED` occurrence without a trade into `RISK_REJECTED`.

Update the occurrence ledger, API schema, generated clients, diagnostics, and UI descriptions consistently.
