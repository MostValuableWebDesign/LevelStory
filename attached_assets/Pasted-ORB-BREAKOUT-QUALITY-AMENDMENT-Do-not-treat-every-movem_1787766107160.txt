ORB BREAKOUT QUALITY AMENDMENT

Do not treat every movement beyond the ORB boundary as a qualified breakout.

The strategy must distinguish between:

1. ORB probe
2. Weak ORB break
3. Qualified ORB breakout
4. Failed ORB breakout
5. Qualified breakout awaiting pullback
6. Pullback and patience-candle setup

WEAK ORB PROBE

Classify the move as ORB_PROBE_WAIT when price:

- Only wicks slightly beyond the ORB boundary
- Trades beyond the boundary intrabar but does not close beyond it
- Closes only marginally beyond the boundary
- Has weak or below-baseline volume
- Shows no directional continuation
- Immediately stalls near the boundary
- Moves back inside the ORB

A weak probe does not qualify the breakout.

Do not:

- Start the pullback sequence
- Search for an entry patience candle
- Enter the trade
- Treat the ORB boundary as successfully broken

Display:

WEAK ORB PROBE — WAIT FOR CONFIRMATION

SLIGHT-BREAK PROXIMITY

Initial configurable slight-break threshold:

Maximum of:

- Two minimum futures ticks
- 0.10 of the 5-minute 14-period ATR

A close beyond the ORB by less than this threshold should remain a weak probe unless unusually strong volume and candle structure support the breakout.

QUALIFIED ORB BREAKOUT

A qualified bullish breakout requires:

- A completed 5-minute candle closes above the ORB high
- The close has meaningful distance beyond the ORB high
- Volume supports the move
- Candle structure shows bullish intent
- Price does not immediately collapse back inside the ORB
- The move demonstrates continuation or acceptance above the ORB

A qualified bearish breakout requires:

- A completed 5-minute candle closes below the ORB low
- The close has meaningful distance beyond the ORB low
- Volume supports the move
- Candle structure shows bearish intent
- Price does not immediately reclaim the ORB
- The move demonstrates continuation or acceptance below the ORB

INITIAL CONFIGURABLE BREAKOUT DEFAULTS

Meaningful close distance:

Maximum of:

- Two minimum futures ticks
- 0.10 of the 5-minute 14-period ATR

Supporting volume:

- Breakout candle volume is at least 1.25 times the average volume of the previous six completed 5-minute candles

Directional candle body:

- Candle body is at least 60% of its total high-low range

Bullish close location:

- Close is in the upper 25% of the breakout candle’s range

Bearish close location:

- Close is in the lower 25% of the breakout candle’s range

These are initial testing values. Keep them configurable and do not present them as proven optimal thresholds.

STRONG CONTINUATION

After the first qualifying close, continuation is confirmed when at least one of these configurable conditions occurs:

- The immediately following candle extends farther in the breakout direction without closing back inside the ORB
- Price achieves additional movement of at least two ticks or 0.10 ATR beyond the qualifying breakout close
- Two consecutive completed candles close outside the ORB
- Price consolidates above the ORB high for a bullish breakout
- Price consolidates below the ORB low for a bearish breakout

Record which continuation condition passed.

PREFERRED INITIAL RULE

Initially require:

1. A qualifying completed candle close outside the ORB
2. Supporting volume
3. Either:
   - Immediate directional extension, or
   - A second completed candle close outside the ORB

Only then change the state to:

QUALIFIED BREAKOUT — WAITING FOR PULLBACK

If the first breakout candle is exceptionally strong, allow the continuation requirement to be satisfied by that candle when all of these pass:

- Volume is at least 1.5 times the recent baseline
- Body is at least 70% of the candle range
- Close is in the outer 20% of its range
- Close distance exceeds the configured meaningful-break threshold

Make this exception configurable.

WAITING AFTER A WEAK BREAK

When price slightly breaks the ORB without sufficient volume or continuation:

- Remain in WAITING state
- Continue monitoring subsequent candles
- Do not permanently invalidate the directional setup
- Require a later strong push to qualify the breakout
- Recalculate breakout volume using the later strong-push candle
- Use the later strong push as the beginning of the impulse leg
- Use that move when selecting Fibonacci anchors

If a later strong push qualifies:

- Mark the new breakout candle and time
- Establish the impulse swing
- Wait for a pullback or retracement
- Require price to reach or come near a qualifying level
- Require a valid patience candle
- Allow only the immediately following candle to trigger entry

Do not use a patience candle that formed before the strong breakout was qualified.

FAILED BREAKOUT

Classify the breakout as failed when:

- A qualifying breakout occurs
- Price then closes back inside the ORB before producing acceptable continuation
- Opposing volume increases materially
- The ORB boundary fails to hold on the retest

Display:

FAILED ORB BREAKOUT — NO CONTINUATION ENTRY

A failed breakout may later be evaluated as a separate reversal setup, but it cannot qualify as an ordinary ORB continuation entry.

REQUIRED SEQUENCE

For a standard ORB continuation trade, enforce this order:

1. ORB completes
2. Price attempts to break the ORB
3. Breakout quality is evaluated
4. Weak probes remain in WAITING
5. A strong push qualifies the breakout
6. Continuation is confirmed
7. Price pulls back or retraces
8. Pullback reaches or comes near a qualifying level
9. A completed patience candle forms
10. Only the immediately following candle can trigger
11. Entry occurs intrabar in the trend direction

Do not allow the application to skip or reorder these steps.

TESTS

Add deterministic tests for:

- Wick above ORB with no close
- Wick below ORB with no close
- Marginal close with weak volume
- Marginal close with no continuation
- Weak probe followed by return inside ORB
- Weak probe followed later by strong bullish push
- Weak probe followed later by strong bearish push
- Strong single-candle breakout exception
- Qualifying breakout followed by continuation
- Qualifying breakout followed by immediate failure
- Pullback beginning only after breakout qualification
- Fibonacci anchors beginning from the later strong push
- Patience candle forming before breakout qualification being rejected
- Valid pullback and patience candle after qualified breakout
