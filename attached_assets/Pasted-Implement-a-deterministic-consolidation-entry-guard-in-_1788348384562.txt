Implement a deterministic consolidation-entry guard in LevelStory.

Goal

Prevent patience-candle entries from being triggered while price remains trapped inside an active tight consolidation zone.

Do not change the underlying patience-candle shape definition, immediate P→E pairing, NTZ rule, eight-tick confirmation buffer, key-level pullback tolerance, stop-loss policy, profit-target policy, or 1:00 p.m. ET entry cutoff.

Authoritative behavior

1. Detect and freeze consolidation

When the existing causal consolidation detector identifies a valid tight/stable range, freeze:

- consolidationZoneHigh
- consolidationZoneLow
- consolidationStartTime
- consolidationDetectionTime
- source candle timestamps
- range width
- detector/configuration version

Only completed, machine-visible five-minute candles may contribute.

The frozen zone must not expand afterward to absorb breakout candles.

2. Patience candles inside consolidation

A patience candle may be detected inside an active consolidation zone, but it is evidence only.

Record it as:

PATIENCE_INSIDE_CONSOLIDATION

It must remain non-executable unless its immediate next completed candle becomes a valid consolidation-breakout entry candle.

3. Direct consolidation-breakout entry

A patience candle inside consolidation may produce an entry only when its immediate next candle E:

- Reaches the directional patience confirmation threshold.
- Uses the configured eight-MES-tick confirmation buffer.
- Closes outside the frozen consolidation zone in the trade direction.
- Is outside the finalized ORB/NTZ.
- Opens before the exclusive 1:00 p.m. ET cutoff.
- Satisfies the Consolidation Breakout Continuation edge.

Long:

E.close > consolidationZoneHigh

Short:

E.close < consolidationZoneLow

A wick outside the zone without a completed close outside does not qualify.

4. Failed immediate confirmation

If immediate E does not close outside the consolidation zone:

- P expires.
- No candidate, entry, fill, or trade may be created from that P.
- Continue scanning for a new patience candle.
- A later candle cannot confirm the expired P.
- A later P2 may only be confirmed by its own immediate E2.

5. Breakout-pullback sequence

After a completed breakout close ends the consolidation:

- Preserve the frozen consolidation boundary as a possible key level.
- Permit a pullback toward the broken boundary or another qualifying key level.
- Require a new patience candle.
- Require its immediate next E confirmation.
- Give this breakout-pullback-patience sequence stronger confluence evidence than a direct breakout.

Do not fabricate a pullback or level interaction.

6. Universal guard

No strategy may bypass the consolidation guard merely because another confluence exists.

If price remains inside an active frozen consolidation zone, no authoritative candidate or trade may be created unless the immediate E candle closes outside the zone under the direct consolidation-breakout rule.

Confluences may increase the setup grade but cannot override this block.

7. Audit evidence

Add explicit deterministic states and reasons:

- CONSOLIDATION_ZONE_FROZEN
- PATIENCE_INSIDE_CONSOLIDATION
- CONSOLIDATION_BREAKOUT_CONFIRMED
- CONSOLIDATION_BREAKOUT_CLOSE_NOT_CONFIRMED
- PATIENCE_EXPIRED_INSIDE_CONSOLIDATION
- BREAKOUT_PULLBACK_PATIENCE_CONFIRMED

Store the exact P, E, zone boundaries, timestamps, direction, threshold, and E closing price.

8. Candidate enforcement

Enforce this before authoritative candidate projection and candidate-owned trade creation.

Rejected signals must not affect:

- Trades
- Fills
- P&L
- Win/loss
- Drawdown
- Fees
- Slippage
- Exit statistics

9. Tests

Add deterministic tests proving:

- A P→E break that remains inside consolidation creates no trade.
- A wick outside followed by a close inside creates no trade.
- An immediate E close outside creates exactly one candidate and trade.
- A failed P1 expires and a valid P2→E2 may qualify.
- A later candle cannot confirm P1.
- Breakout → pullback → new P→E can qualify.
- Another edge or confluence cannot bypass the active-zone guard.
- Entries at or after 1:00 p.m. ET remain prohibited.
- No future candle is used to define or modify the frozen zone.

Verification

Run only focused consolidation, patience, candidate-projection, and directly affected execution tests, plus:

- API typecheck
- git diff --check

Do not run a historical-index rebuild, broad qualification batch, or cold multi-contract replay.
