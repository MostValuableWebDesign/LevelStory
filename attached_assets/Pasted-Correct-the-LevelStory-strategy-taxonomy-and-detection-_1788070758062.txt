Correct the LevelStory strategy taxonomy and detection architecture. The current interface incorrectly treats setup components and trade outcomes as separate strategy edges.

Authoritative clarification:

My independent strategy edges are:

1. ORB pullback continuation
2. Consolidation breakout continuation
3. Equivalent-candle reversal

A patience candle is the universal entry trigger used by every strategy. A pullback, consolidation, breakout, stop, target, or runner exit is not an independent strategy.

STRATEGY 1 — ORB PULLBACK CONTINUATION

Required causal sequence:

A. A valid completed-candle breakout outside the finalized ORB/NTZ.
B. Breakout direction agrees with the directional trend.
C. Price pulls back within the configured 12-tick area of at least one qualifying level.
D. A trend-aligned patience candle P forms.
E. The immediately following completed five-minute candle confirms P by the configured 3–4 tick entry buffer.
F. Risk controls approve the entry.
G. Only then may a simulated entry be recorded.

Support both directions:

Long:
ORB/NTZ breakout upward → pullback → bullish P → immediate E breaks above P.

Short:
ORB/NTZ breakout downward → pullback → bearish P → immediate E breaks below P.

Do not create separate strategy IDs for bullish and bearish patience. Store direction as long or short under the ORB pullback continuation strategy.

STRATEGY 2 — CONSOLIDATION BREAKOUT CONTINUATION

Required causal sequence:

A. A valid consolidation exists.
B. Price produces a strong directional breakout supported by volume and continuation.
C. After the breakout, either:
   - Price pulls back to a qualifying level area; or
   - Price forms a valid post-breakout consolidation when no pullback occurs.
D. A trend-aligned patience candle P forms.
E. The immediately following five-minute E candle confirms P using the configured entry buffer.
F. Risk controls approve the entry.
G. Only then may a simulated entry be recorded.

A strong breakout alone is not a trade and is not a complete edge.

Rename the current “Strong breakout” strategy tab to:

Consolidation breakout

The complete strategy must include the patience-entry sequence.

STRATEGY 3 — EQUIVALENT-CANDLE REVERSAL

Required causal sequence:

A. Equivalent opposing full-body candles occur at or within the qualifying area of a major level.
B. The candles satisfy the configured body-size and wick tolerances.
C. Reversal context is recorded.
D. A directionally appropriate patience candle P forms.
E. The immediate E candle confirms P.
F. Risk controls approve the entry.
G. Only then may a simulated reversal entry be recorded.

Do not enter solely because equivalent candles were detected.
