Separate historical pattern detection from trade qualification and retain every causal occurrence during the selected session.

Current problem:
The pullback detector examines only a short six-candle / 30-minute post-breakout window. Pullbacks and patience candles that occur later in the session are missed or discarded when entry confirmation fails.

Requirements:

1. Create a session-wide occurrence detector for the selected review window:
   - Primary trading window: 9:30 a.m.–1:00 p.m. America/New_York.
   - Permit full regular-session review through 4:00 p.m. when explicitly selected.
   - Process completed 5-minute candles only.
   - Preserve contract, trading date, and causal-boundary isolation.

2. Scan and retain every occurrence of:
   - Pullback into a qualifying 12-tick level area
   - Consolidation near a qualifying level
   - Bullish patience candle
   - Bearish patience candle
   - Immediate next-candle confirmation
   - Failed confirmation
   - Invalidation
   - Qualified trade

3. Detection stages must be independent:
   Stage A: directional 15-minute trend
   Stage B: ORB/NTZ breakout or valid consolidation-breakout context
   Stage C: pullback to a qualifying level area
   Stage D: patience candle
   Stage E: immediately following confirmation candle
   Stage F: risk approval
   Stage G: simulated trade qualification

4. Preserve evidence when later stages fail.
   Examples:
   - Pullback found but no patience candle
   - Patience candle found but next candle did not confirm
   - Confirmation found but risk controls rejected the trade
   These must remain reviewable historical occurrences.

5. Apply my patience-candle definition:
   - A bullish patience candle requires a bullish trend.
   - Its trend-direction high must remain inside/not exceed the preceding candle’s high.
   - A bearish patience candle requires a bearish trend.
   - Its trend-direction low must remain inside/not exceed the preceding candle’s low.
   - The entire candle does not have to be an inside candle.

6. Entry confirmation:
   - E must be the immediately following completed 5-minute candle after P.
   - Long: E must break above P’s high by the configured entry confirmation buffer.
   - Short: E must break below P’s low by the configured entry confirmation buffer.
   - A later candle cannot replace the immediately following E candle.
   - Keep the 12-tick pullback-area tolerance separate from the entry confirmation buffer.

7. Store occurrences using stable deterministic identifiers containing:
   - Contract
   - New York trading date
   - Event type
   - L timestamp
   - P timestamp when available
   - E timestamp when available
   - Direction
   - Qualifying level and value at L

8. Do not change trading rules merely to increase the number of trades. The goal is complete detection and evidence preservation.

Add focused tests proving:
- Multiple pullbacks can be retained in one session.
- Multiple patience candles can be retained in one session.
- A failed entry confirmation does not erase P or L.
- A later candle cannot confirm an earlier P.
- Occurrences after the old six-candle/30-minute boundary are retained.
- Duplicate scans produce identical stable IDs without duplicate records.

Do not run a full historical-index rebuild or a long qualification batch.
