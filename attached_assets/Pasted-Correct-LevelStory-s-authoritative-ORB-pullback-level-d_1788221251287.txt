Problem demonstrated by the supplied example:

1. Price broke below the finalized ORB/NTZ.
2. Price pulled back toward multiple causal key levels.
3. The pullback was within 12 MES ticks of levels including the 200 EMA, VWAP, premarket low, ORB/NTZ low, and major support/resistance.
4. A bearish patience candle formed.
5. Its immediate next candle confirmed the short entry.
6. After entry, price moved favorably and came within 12 ticks of the next major support/resistance level around 6773.
7. The system failed to preserve the pullback confluences and failed to take the key-level profit, eventually turning a winning trade into a loss.

MES math:

- Tick size: 0.25
- 12 ticks: 3.00 points
- Eight entry-confirmation ticks remain 2.00 points
- Do not confuse the 12-tick pullback/target proximity with the eight-tick entry buffer

PART 1 — AUTHORITATIVE 12-TICK PULLBACK DETECTION

For every possible pullback candle L after a completed directional ORB/NTZ breakout:

1. Evaluate its complete high-low wick range.
2. Resolve every eligible level using only information available at L.
3. Dynamic levels must use their value at L:
   - VWAP at L
   - 200 EMA at L
4. Static levels must come from the causal session snapshot:
   - ORB/NTZ high and low
   - Premarket high and low
   - Previous-day high and low
   - Two-days-ago high and low
   - Major support/resistance zones
   - Dynamite zones
5. Never use the final-session VWAP, final-session EMA, or a later audit value to validate an earlier pullback.
6. A pullback qualifies when the distance between the level and the L candle’s full high-low range is at most 12 ticks.

For a point level:

```text
distance =
  0, when level is between L.low and L.high
  L.low - level, when level is below L.low
  level - L.high, when level is above L.high

qualifies when distance <= 3.00 MES points
For a level zone [zoneLow, zoneHigh]:
distance =
  0, when the L candle range overlaps the zone
  L.low - zoneHigh, when the zone is below the candle
  zoneLow - L.high, when the zone is above the candle

qualifies when distance <= 3.00 MES points
Important:
The pullback may contain one candle or multiple candles.
The patience candle may itself be the qualifying pullback candle.
At least one eligible causal level is required.
Do not require every nearby level to qualify.
Preserve every qualifying level as supporting confluence.
Deduplicate aliases such as ORB low and NTZ low when they represent the same physical price.
Multiple levels near the same price should increase the setup grade and may form a dynamite zone, but they must produce only one physical trade.
Do not discard a valid earlier pullback because a later audit cursor observes it again.
Do not let a later pullback overwrite the L/P/E sequence that actually produced the confirmed entry.

PART 2 — FREEZE THE PROFIT-TARGET LEVEL SET AT ENTRY
At the completed E candle, freeze an immutable target-level snapshot using only levels visible at that time.
The snapshot must include:
Point levels
Major support/resistance zones
Dynamite zones
Previous-day and two-days-ago levels
Premarket levels
ORB/NTZ levels
Any other governed structural level eligible for profit-taking
Do not introduce a target later using future-derived information.
Do not require the target level to be one of the pullback confluences. The pullback level explains entry; the next directional key level explains profit-taking.
PART 3 — SELECT THE NEXT DIRECTIONAL PROFIT LEVEL
For a long trade:
Consider eligible resistance levels or zones above entry.
Ignore any whose nearest boundary is within 12 ticks of entry.
Select the nearest remaining level in the profitable direction.
For a short trade:
Consider eligible support levels or zones below entry.
Ignore any whose nearest boundary is within 12 ticks of entry.
Select the nearest remaining level in the profitable direction.
When levels overlap or are within the governed dynamite tolerance, treat the combined area as one target zone. Do not generate multiple targets for the same physical area.
Persist:
target level ID
target type
point value or zone bounds
distance from entry
skipped-near-entry levels and reasons
selected directional target
target execution threshold
snapshot observation timestamp
PART 4 — EXIT WITHIN 12 TICKS OF THE TARGET
The trader does not require price to touch the exact level.
For a short approaching support from above:
point target threshold = support price + 3.00
zone target threshold = support zone high + 3.00
exit when a completed post-entry candle low <= threshold
For a long approaching resistance from below:
point target threshold = resistance price - 3.00
zone target threshold = resistance zone low - 3.00
exit when a completed post-entry candle high >= threshold
Model the profit exit at the deterministic threshold price, not at an invented better price.
If price reaches or crosses the actual level, the buffered threshold was necessarily reached first. Use the buffered threshold unless existing lower-resolution OHLC ambiguity requires the established conservative ambiguity policy.
Exit replay must:
Begin after entry has been established
Never use a candle before the entry observation
Preserve existing stop-versus-target ambiguity handling
Produce one finalized exit
Prevent a later stop from replacing an earlier target exit
Calculate realized MES P/L, fees, and slippage from the authoritative entry and exit
Draw entry, target, and final exit lines on Visual Review
Show the selected target level, 12-tick buffer, exit reason, and realized P/L
