The Phase 3 architecture is solid, but I would not run the full 20+10-session pilot yet. Two causal-timing issues and one management issue could distort results.

Critical findings:

1. The 1:00 p.m. cutoff uses the E candle’s opening timestamp. A 12:55–1:00 candle could therefore qualify even though confirmation is not known until 1:00. The cutoff must use E close/entry-observation time and require it to be strictly before 1:00 p.m. ET.

2. Phase 9 records the modeled entry at E open even though E’s high/low confirms the threshold only after E completes. This backdates the entry five minutes. Store E open separately, but use E close as `entryObservationTimestamp`, modeled fill observation time, trade entry time, time bucket, and cutoff timestamp.

3. Phase 3 filters invalid stop/target geometry from scoring, but Phase 9 can still send invalid geometry into outcome simulation. Core candidate execution must validate target, strategy stop, catastrophe stop, runner settings, and contract quantity before management replay.

4. The Phase 3 manifest says physical identity includes `lTimestamp`, while the canonical P→E identity should not be split by qualifying level. The actual Phase 3 deduplication key also omits direction. Use:

```text
source fingerprint
formula/configuration hash
contract
New York trading date
direction
P open timestamp
immediate E open timestamp
```
