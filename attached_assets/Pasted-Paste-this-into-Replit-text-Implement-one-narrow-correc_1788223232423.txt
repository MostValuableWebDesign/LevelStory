Implement one narrow correctness pass for LevelStory’s causal ORB pullback lifecycle.

The previous change correctly removed the six-candle/30-minute eligibility expiration, but it left the original breakout active across every later candle until 1:00 p.m. That can incorrectly associate unrelated later candles with a stale breakout.

Do not make frontend changes. Do not rebuild the historical index. Do not change stops, target placement, broker behavior, or live/paper trading.

PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Replace the unrestricted all-morning pullback scan with an explicit causal lifecycle:

ORB breakout
→ pullback monitoring
→ qualifying-level interaction
→ patience candle
→ immediate next-candle confirmation
→ candidate consumed

or a deterministic terminal state.

1. ADD AN AUTHORITATIVE PULLBACK-ARM STATE MACHINE

Use explicit lifecycle states similar to:

- ARMED_AFTER_BREAKOUT
- PULLBACK_OBSERVED
- LEVEL_INTERACTION_FOUND
- PATIENCE_ARMED
- SIGNAL_CONFIRMED
- CONSUMED
- STRUCTURALLY_INVALIDATED
- SUPERSEDED_BY_NEW_BREAKOUT
- OPPOSITE_BREAKOUT_INVALIDATED
- ENTRY_CUTOFF_EXPIRED
- SESSION_BOUNDARY_EXPIRED
- CONTRACT_BOUNDARY_EXPIRED

State transitions must be causal and monotonic.

A terminal arm must never return to an active state.

Repeated audit observations must reference the existing arm rather than creating duplicate terminal transitions.

2. REMOVE STALE-BREAKOUT ASSOCIATIONS

After a completed ORB/NTZ breakout, keep its arm active beyond the old six-candle/30-minute diagnostics, but end or supersede it when any authoritative event occurs:

- a confirmed candidate consumes it;
- a newer completed breakout supersedes the prior active breakout;
- a completed opposite-direction breakout is established;
- an existing governed structural-invalidation predicate becomes true;
- the contract changes;
- the New York trading date/session changes;
- the exclusive 1:00 p.m. ET entry cutoff is reached.

Do not invent arbitrary elapsed-time expiration.

Do not invalidate a setup merely because a normal pullback touches or briefly re-enters the ORB boundary. Entry E must be outside the finalized NTZ, but the pullback itself may interact with the ORB/NTZ boundary.

If the codebase does not already contain a governed structural-invalidation definition, do not invent a price threshold. Record that limitation and rely on the other explicit terminal events above.

3. SUPPORT MULTIPLE INDEPENDENT SEQUENCES

A session may contain more than one valid breakout/pullback sequence.

Each sequence must have a deterministic arm identity based on causal facts such as:

- source fingerprint;
- formula/configuration hash;
- contract;
- New York trading date;
- breakout direction;
- breakout candle timestamp;
- finalized ORB/NTZ identity.

A later breakout must receive a new arm identity.

A trade created from one arm must not consume, inherit, or reuse:

- another arm’s level interaction;
- another arm’s P or E;
- another trade’s stop;
- another trade’s target plan.

4. RETAIN THE 12-TICK LEVEL-INTERACTION RULE

A trade candidate still requires an actual machine-visible qualifying-level interaction during the pullback.

At least one pullback candle, including P when applicable, must touch, cross, or come within 12 MES ticks of:

- ORB/NTZ high or low;
- VWAP at that candle;
- 200 EMA at that candle;
- premarket high or low;
- previous-day high or low;
- two-days-ago high or low;
- major support/resistance zone;
- dynamite zone.

Use candle wick/range distance:

- overlap or crossing = zero distance;
- otherwise use the nearest high/low-to-level or zone-boundary distance.

Persist the exact interaction candle, causal level value/zone, distance, level identity, and source timestamp.

Do not infer qualifying levels after E.

5. PRESERVE PATIENCE REARMING

Only the immediate next completed candle can confirm P.

- P1 can only be confirmed by E1.
- If E1 fails, P1 expires.
- Continue scanning within the same still-active pullback arm.
- A later P2 can only be confirmed by E2.
- E2 cannot confirm P1.
- Once a P→E signal confirms and creates a candidate, consume that arm.
- Do not let later audit cursors overwrite the confirmed P/E pair.

P may also be the one and only pullback candle when it interacts with a qualifying level.

No P or E may qualify inside the finalized NTZ.

E must open before 1:00 p.m. ET.

6. KEEP ORB AND CONSOLIDATION EDGES INDEPENDENT

ORB_BREAK_PULLBACK_PATIENCE_CONTINUATION must not require every strong-breakout-after-consolidation predicate.

ORB requires:

- finalized ORB/NTZ;
- completed directional breakout outside it;
- adequate directional continuation under the governed ORB rule;
- causal pullback;
- qualifying-level interaction;
- P;
- immediate E confirmation;
- E outside NTZ;
- E before 1:00 p.m. ET.

Volume, candle body, and close-location may support confluence/grade unless the governed ORB definition explicitly requires them.

The tight-range, strong-body, and strong-volume gates remain authoritative for STRONG_BREAKOUT_AFTER_CONSOLIDATION only.

7. CORRECT THE LATE-PULLBACK TEST

Replace or strengthen the current synthetic test.

The early post-breakout candles must remain more than 12 ticks from every qualifying level.

Only a candle occurring after:

- more than six completed five-minute candles; and
- more than 30 elapsed minutes

should first enter the 12-tick level range.

Prove that this later candle is recorded as the first qualifying interaction.

The test must fail under the former six-candle/30-minute truncation.

8. ADD FOCUSED LIFECYCLE TESTS

Add deterministic tests proving:

A. A first level interaction after 30 minutes remains eligible.

B. A newer same-direction breakout supersedes the older arm and receives a new identity.

C. An opposite completed breakout invalidates the prior arm.

D. A consumed arm cannot generate another candidate.

E. P1→E1 failure rearms, and later P2→E2 may confirm.

F. A later candle cannot confirm an earlier failed P.

G. One-candle pullback-as-P is supported.

H. A P→E pair without a persisted 12-tick level interaction creates no candidate.

I. E inside NTZ creates no candidate.

J. E at or after 1:00 p.m. ET creates no candidate.

K. Contract and trading-date boundaries terminate the arm.

L. Repeated audit observations do not inflate transition counts.

M. Separate arms produce separate P-derived stops and separately frozen E-time target plans.

N. ORB qualification does not inherit consolidation-only strength predicates.

9. ADD A GENERAL HISTORICAL REGRESSION FIXTURE

Add a reusable deterministic fixture representing:

- downside ORB breakout;
- early candles that do not interact with a level;
- first valid pullback interaction occurring after 30 minutes;
- pullback within 12 ticks of machine-visible key-level evidence;
- bearish P;
- immediate E confirms below P by the governed eight-tick buffer;
- E outside NTZ and before 1:00 p.m.;
- candidate and fill belong to the same arm;
- frozen target selects the next eligible lower key level;
- target is reached before the trade-specific stop.

Do not hardcode the production logic to a particular date or price.

10. DIAGNOSTICS

Report counts for:

- arms created;
- active arms;
- superseded arms;
- opposite-breakout invalidations;
- structural invalidations;
- consumed arms;
- cutoff/session/contract expirations;
- duplicate transition references removed;
- late pullback interactions found after the old diagnostic limit;
- confirmed signals and candidates per arm;
- invariant violations.

Required invariants:

- at most one authoritative candidate per physical P→E signal;
- at most one consumed transition per arm;
- no candidate belongs to a terminal arm unless that same candidate caused CONSUMED;
- no candidate after 1:00 p.m. ET;
- no candidate without a qualifying-level interaction;
- no trade inherits another arm’s P, stop, or target.

11. COMPUTE-CONSCIOUS VERIFICATION

Run only:

- focused Phase 4, Phase 5, Phase 6, Phase 9, target-planning, and OHLCV tests;
- API typecheck;

Do not:

12. FINAL REPORT
