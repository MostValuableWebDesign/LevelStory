- Completed close below ORB/NTZ low establishes bearish local direction.
- This direction persists for the causal breakout/pullback sequence until structurally invalidated.
- A neutral or opposing 15-minute trend does not block this edge.
- Record the 15-minute trend as supporting, neutral, or opposing confluence for grading.

B. STRONG_BREAKOUT_AFTER_CONSOLIDATION

- The confirmed consolidation breakout establishes local direction.
- Fifteen-minute trend is confluence, not a universal blocker.

C. EQUIVALENT_CANDLE_REVERSAL

- The confirmed reversal pattern establishes reversal direction.

D. PATIENCE_CANDLE_CONTINUATION

- ORB_BREAKOUT
