Objective

- A prior-direction patience entry
- The reversal-confirming candle

Required causal policy

Tests must enforce this ordering:

1. The prior ORB direction remains active throughout the reversal-confirming candle.
2. The reversal is confirmed only when that candle completes completes.
3. A prior-direction threshold entry reached threshold reached at the confirming candle’s open has known ordering and may confirm.
4. A prior-direction threshold reached only intrabar on that candle, when ordering cannot be determined from five-minute OHLC, becomes `AMBIGUOUS_EVENT_ORDER`.
5. A pending prior-direction occurrence that never reaches its threshold is expired with `ORB_TREND_REVERSED`.
6. The new ORB direction becomes active only on the immediately following candle.
7. One occurrence can never be both confirmed and expired.

1. Phase 4 tests

Add direct tests for `deferTerminalAtCandleOpenTime`.

Test A — defer reversal terminal on the confirming candle:

- Begin with an active bullish pullback arm.
- Supply a completed candle closing beyond the bearish ORB reversal boundary.
- Pass that candle’s open time as `deferTerminalAtCandleOpenTime`.
- Assert that Phase 4 does not terminate the prior arm from that candle before Phase 5 evaluates it.
- Assert the candle remains present in the prior epoch’s evaluated window.

Test B — normal behavior without deferral:

- Use the same fixture without `deferTerminalAtCandleOpenTime`.
- Assert the arm terminates with the expected opposite-breakout or ORB-reentry state.

Test C — deferral is candle-specific:

- Add a later independent terminal candle.
- Assert only the designated confirming candle is deferred.
- Assert the later candle can still terminate the arm normally.

Test D — independent earlier expiration remains authoritative:

- Expire the arm through a session boundary, contract boundary, data gap, or entry cutoff before the reversal.
- Assert the reversal deferral does not revive the arm.

2. Phase 5 tests

Create a deterministic prior-direction P→E fixture where E is also the completed candle that confirms the opposite ORB direction.

Test A — threshold reached at E open:

For a prior bullish direction:

- P creates a long confirmation threshold.
- E opens at or above that threshold.
- E later closes below the bearish ORB reversal boundary.
- Assert entry ordering is known at E open.
- Assert the occurrence is `CONFIRMED`.
- Assert its qualification is `SIGNAL_CONFIRMED`.
- Assert it is not changed to `INVALIDATED`.
- Assert its ID does not appear in reversal-expired candidates.

Add the mirrored bearish case if practical.

Test B — threshold reached only intrabar:

- E opens below the prior bullish threshold.
- E’s high reaches the threshold.
- E later closes below the bearish reversal boundary.
- With only five-minute OHLC, the entry-versus-reversal sequence is unknown.
- Assert state is `AMBIGUOUS_EVENT_ORDER`.
- Assert qualification is `STRUCTURALLY_INVALIDATED`.
- Assert no confirmed trade is produced.
- Assert the occurrence is not also labeled as an ordinary pending reversal expiration.
- Assert the ambiguity explanation mentions same-candle ordering.

Test C — threshold never reached:

- E never reaches the prior-direction threshold.
- E closes beyond the opposite ORB boundary.
- Assert the occurrence remains pending through E processing.
- Assert it becomes `INVALIDATED` at `effectiveFromTimestamp`.
- Assert the reason is `ORB_TREND_REVERSED`.

Test D — no reversal:

- Use a normal immediate E that reaches the threshold without confirming an ORB reversal.
- Assert existing entry behavior remains unchanged.

3. ORB state-machine timing test

Add or strengthen a test proving:

- On the reversal-confirming candle’s open, `trendDirectionAt()` returns the prior direction.
- At the confirming candle’s close/effective timestamp, the transition becomes effective.
- At the immediately following candle’s open, `trendDirectionAt()` returns the new direction.
- The confirming candle cannot act as the first P or E candle of the new epoch.

5. Invariant assertions

For every fixture, assert:

- No candidate has both `SIGNAL_CONFIRMED` and `ORB_TREND_REVERSED`.
- No candidate has both `CONFIRMED` and `INVALIDATED`.
- No duplicate candidate IDs.
- No duplicate expired IDs.
- Candidate epoch ID matches the direction active at its candle open.
- The new epoch produces no evidence before its effective timestamp.
- Only completed candles visible at the evaluation cursor are used.
- Changing a candle after the evaluation cursor does not alter the result.

6. Avoid brittle tests

- Use deterministic candle fixtures.
- Prefer explicit timestamps and prices.
- Do not depend on random simulated-feed seeds for the core ordering cases.
- Do not assert entire large objects when focused field assertions are clearer.
- Keep identifiers stable through the actual production identity functions.
- Do not loosen production rules merely to make fixtures qualify.
