1. FIX OCCURRENCE-SPECIFIC EVALUATION CURSORS

Current problem:

`buildPatienceOccurrences` can fall back to the last completed candle in the visible prefix when the immediate E candle is missing or incomplete. This can give an earlier occurrence a later evaluation cursor.

Every occurrence must stop at the earliest causal time when its state became knowable.

Required cursor rules:

Confirmed P → E:

- E must be the immediate five-minute interval after P.
- `evaluationCursor = E.closeTime`.

Completed E that fails confirmation:

- Mark P as `EXPIRED_NO_IMMEDIATE_CONFIRMATION`, `EXPIRED_WRONG_DIRECTION`, or `INVALIDATED`.
- `evaluationCursor = E.closeTime`.

Missing immediate E interval:

- Mark P as `EXPIRED_MISSING_E` once a later interval proves the expected E interval is absent.
- Use the expected E interval boundary as the occurrence cursor.
- Do not use the later observed candle’s close.
- Store the later candle only as diagnostic `nextObservedCandle`.
- Never label it E.

Incomplete immediate E with no later interval:

- Keep status as `CANDIDATE`.
- Do not invent E.
- The cursor must not extend past currently available causal evidence.

Incomplete immediate E followed by a later interval:

- Mark P as `EXPIRED_INCOMPLETE_E`.
- Use the expected immediate E interval boundary as the occurrence cursor.
- Do not use the later completed candle’s close.
- Do not allow the later candle to confirm P.

No immediate candle yet:

- Keep P as `CANDIDATE`.
- Use P close or the exact current causal cursor, whichever is valid without future access.

Remove any fallback equivalent to:

`completed.at(-1)?.closeTime`

when assigning an individual occurrence’s cursor.

Visual Review must continue using the occurrence cursor rather than the surrounding audit prefix.

Add deterministic tests proving:

- Missing E followed by a later candle does not advance the occurrence cursor.
- Incomplete E followed by a later candle does not advance the occurrence cursor.
- A failed completed E uses exactly E close.
- A confirmed E uses exactly E close.
- Mutating candles after the occurrence cursor cannot change the occurrence or its snapshot.
- Machine-visible candles never extend beyond the occurrence cursor.

3. MODEL A GENUINE PULLBACK INDEPENDENTLY OF ITS LEVEL

Current problem:

ORB currently treats a “genuine pullback” as an interaction with a non-Fibonacci level. This prevents a real pullback that interacts only with a Fibonacci level from qualifying.

Correct the model by separating:

A. Pullback structure

B. Qualifying L-level interaction

A genuine pullback must be detected from causal price movement after the completed ORB/NTZ breakout.

For a bullish breakout, the pullback should show countertrend downward retracement from a causal post-breakout peak.

For a bearish breakout, the pullback should show countertrend upward retracement from a causal post-breakout trough.

The pullback detector must identify:

- Breakout direction
- Causal impulse peak or trough
- Countertrend retracement
- L candle
- Pullback depth
- Pullback start and end
- No future candles
- Whether the move became a greater-than-50% reversal warning

Do not define “genuine pullback” merely as “a non-Fibonacci level event exists.”

After detecting the structural pullback, require its exact L candle to interact with at least one qualifying level:

- ORB/NTZ
- VWAP
- EMA 200
- Premarket high/low
- Prior-day high/low
- Day-before-prior high/low
- Major support/resistance
- Valid frozen Fibonacci retracement level

Use the governed 12-MES-tick tolerance.

Fibonacci rules:

- A random continuation candle near Fibonacci is not a pullback.
- A structurally detected pullback whose L candle interacts with Fibonacci can qualify even if no non-Fibonacci level also qualifies.
- Fibonacci levels must be frozen causally from the appropriate impulse anchors.
- Do not calculate or expose future-dependent Fibonacci levels.
- Preserve all same-L confluences when multiple levels qualify.

ORB qualification must independently require:

1. Finalized ORB/NTZ.
2. Completed breakout outside ORB/NTZ.
3. Genuine countertrend pullback structure.
4. L interaction with at least one qualifying level.
5. Valid patience P after L.
6. Immediate next-candle E confirmation.
7. Configured confirmation buffer.
8. Risk approval.

Required tests:

- Fibonacci proximity without a countertrend pullback fails.
- Countertrend pullback without any qualifying level fails.
- Genuine pullback to Fibonacci alone passes the pullback-and-level gates.
- Genuine pullback to VWAP passes.
- Genuine pullback to EMA 200 passes.
- Genuine pullback to ORB/NTZ passes.
- Genuine pullback to support/resistance passes.
- Multiple same-L levels are preserved as confluence.
- Strong-breakout classification is not required for ORB pullback continuation.
- Pullback and L occur after the breakout and before P.
- P and immediate E occur after L.

4. FIX EQUIVALENT-REVERSAL PATIENCE WIRING

Current problem:

The production path calculates the reversal direction but calls the patience engine using the original continuation trend. A bearish reversal during a bullish continuation trend can therefore be rejected as `PATIENCE_TREND_MISMATCH`.

Do not pass the original continuation trend to reversal patience as if the reversal were another continuation trade.

Create explicit reversal-direction evidence.

Equivalent Candle Reversal must require:

- Equivalent opposing full-body candle pattern.
- Pattern at or within tolerance of a qualifying level.
- Governed causal reversal-direction confirmation.
- Reversal patience candle P aligned with the confirmed reversal direction.
- Immediate next-candle E confirmation.
- Configured buffer.
- Risk approval.

The reversal direction may oppose the preceding 15-minute continuation trend.

Required production behavior:

- If continuation trend is bullish and reversal evidence confirms short:
  - Reversal patience evaluates against confirmed short reversal direction.
  - It is not rejected merely because the earlier continuation trend was bullish.

- If continuation trend is bearish and reversal evidence confirms long:
  - Reversal patience evaluates against confirmed long reversal direction.

Do not simply assign the opposite direction.

Direction must be supported by completed causal reversal evidence, such as:

- Equivalent opposing candles
- Major-level rejection
- Failed breakout
- Structure break
- Strong opposing volume

Create a reversal-specific context or engine mode rather than weakening continuation patience trend requirements.

Continuation patience must still require confirmed causal 15-minute trend evidence.

Required tests:

- Bullish continuation trend with confirmed bearish equivalent reversal can complete bearish P → E.
- Bearish continuation trend with confirmed bullish equivalent reversal can complete bullish P → E.
- Synthetic opposite direction without reversal evidence fails.
- Equivalent pattern without reversal confirmation fails.
- Reversal confirmation without patience fails.
- Reversal P without immediate E fails.
- Valid reversal evidence plus P → immediate E plus risk approval qualifies.
- Generic Patience Continuation cannot claim the reversal trade.
