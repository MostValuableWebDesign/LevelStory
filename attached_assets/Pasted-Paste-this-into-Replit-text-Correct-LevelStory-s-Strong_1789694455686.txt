Correct LevelStory’s Strong Breakout qualification and compact qualification funnel.

AUTHORITATIVE RULE

A Strong Breakout candidate does not require a patience candle.

Patience-candle evidence must never determine whether the strong_breakout_candidate stage passes or fails.

This applies both to strategy qualification and to compact-funnel attribution. Do not merely change displayed wording while leaving patience as a hidden gate.

STRONG BREAKOUT CANDIDATE STAGE

Evaluate the strong breakout candidate only from evidence that belongs to the breakout itself, including the strategy’s governed requirements such as:

- Causal trend direction.
- Directional price movement.
- Required breakout boundary.
- Required eight-tick breakout distance where applicable.
- Valid completed or intrabar crossing evidence according to the strategy contract.
- Causal time and session eligibility.
- Any breakout-specific body, close-location, or volume rule that is genuinely mandatory for that strategy.

Do not require:

- A patience candle.
- A patience candle closing outside the ORB or NTZ.
- An immediate patience trigger.
- A pullback.
- A two-candle continuation pattern.
- A completed later entry sequence.

Those belong to later strategy-specific stages, if the selected strategy requires them.

STRATEGY-SPECIFIC ROUTING

Keep breakout detection separate from downstream entry qualification.

For strategies that require pullback and patience after a breakout:

1. Strong breakout candidate may pass without patience.
2. Later pullback/consolidation stage may reject.
3. Later patience-candle stage may reject.
4. Immediate-trigger stage may reject.

For Strong Breakout After Consolidation:

- Do not require a patience candle at any point.
- Require the independently established causal trend.
- Require at least four completed consolidation candles.
- Require the governed consolidation-quality rules.
- Bullish entry threshold = frozen range HIGH + 8 instrument ticks.
- Bearish entry threshold = frozen range LOW − 8 instrument ticks.
- The modeled fill must be strictly outside the frozen range.
- Bullish stop = frozen range LOW − 8 ticks.
- Bearish stop = frozen range HIGH + 8 ticks.

For Equivalent-Candle Reversal:

- Preserve its own two-candle pattern and immediate trigger rules.
- Do not report its missing pattern as a Strong Breakout candidate failure.

Do not loosen patience requirements for strategies whose later entry contract genuinely requires patience. Move those failures to the correct later funnel stage.

COMPACT QUALIFICATION FUNNEL

Fix the funnel so every primary rejection is attributed to the earliest actual failed requirement.

The strong_breakout_candidate stage must not emit reasons such as:

- No qualifying patience candle.
- Patience candle did not close outside the finalized ORB.
- Waiting for valid patience context.
- Immediate patience trigger unavailable.
- No equivalent opposing-candle structure.
- No eligible two-candle continuation pattern.

Examples of valid strong-breakout rejection reasons include:

- Required breakout boundary was not crossed.
- Required eight-tick breakout distance was not reached.
- Breakout direction conflicted with the causal trend.
- Required breakout evidence was missing or noncausal.
- Breakout occurred outside the authorized session window.
- A genuinely mandatory breakout-quality rule failed.

Route other failures to the correct stage:

- Missing pullback or consolidation context → pullback_or_consolidation.
- Missing required level interaction → critical_level_interaction.
- Missing patience candle → valid_trend_aligned_patience_candle.
- Missing immediate trigger → immediate_next_candle_confirmation.
- Equivalent-candle structure failure → its strategy-specific continuation/reversal stage.
- Invalid stop, target, or reward-to-risk → risk_approved.
- Active account position → account arbitration, after candidate creation.

A single occurrence must have one primary rejection stage. Diagnostic details may show supporting failures, but later failures must not replace the earliest causal blocker.

Do not combine unrelated failures into misleading diagnostic bundles.

REPORTING CORRECTION

When a user asks why no trades were generated:

- First report whether the selected date range actually generated trades.

- Describe the top qualification blockers as reasons other observed setups did not become trades.
- Do not claim that no trades were generated when trades exist.
- Keep qualification rejection counts separate from account-position blocks.

3. Strong breakout followed by missing immediate trigger:
   - Breakout and patience stages pass.
