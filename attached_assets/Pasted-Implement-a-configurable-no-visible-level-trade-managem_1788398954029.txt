Implement a configurable no-visible-level trade-management rule in LevelStory.

CONTEXT

This rule applies only when, at entry time, there is no eligible causal key level visible ahead of the trade in the trade direction.

Do not search future candles for a level. Freeze the no-level determination using only information available at entry.

AUTHORITATIVE RULE

When no eligible forward key level is available:

1. Set the initial profit target to +1R.
2. Preserve the trade’s existing initial protective stop.
3. Count completed five-minute candles after the entry candle.
4. The entry candle is not candle 1.
5. If price reaches +1R before the time condition, exit at +1R.
6. If nine completed post-entry candles pass without reaching +1R, activate a breakeven stop at the original modeled entry price.
7. Do not fabricate a breakeven fill when the ninth candle completes.
8. A breakeven exit occurs only if a subsequent candle genuinely reaches or crosses the entry price.
9. Never move the stop backward or increase risk.
10. Exit any remaining position according to the existing exclusive 1:00 p.m. ET rule.

Nine five-minute candles equal 45 minutes. Make this governed and configurable:

noLevelBreakevenActivationBars = 9

Clarify “whichever comes first” as:

- +1R target fill before breakeven activation, or
- after activation, the first causally observed fill of either the +1R target or breakeven stop.

The breakeven stop and +1R target may remain active together after the ninth candle.

DIRECTIONAL BEHAVIOR

Long:

- +1R target is above entry.
- Breakeven stop is at entry.
- After breakeven activation, a later candle with low at or below entry may fill the breakeven stop.

Short:

- +1R target is below entry.
- Breakeven stop is at entry.
- After breakeven activation, a later candle with high at or above entry may fill the breakeven stop.

SAME-CANDLE AMBIGUITY

If a post-activation candle reaches both the +1R target and breakeven stop and intrabar ordering is unavailable:

- Preserve the existing conservative OHLC ambiguity policy.
- Label the result explicitly.
- Do not assume the target happened first.

TESTS

Add focused deterministic long and short tests proving:

1. +1R reached before nine bars exits at +1R.
2. The entry candle is excluded from the nine-bar count.
3. Nine completed post-entry candles activate breakeven without fabricating an exit.
4. Price later returns to entry and fills the breakeven stop.
5. Price never returns to entry and remains managed until another valid exit.
6. The original protective stop remains active before breakeven activation.
7. Breakeven never widens the original stop.
8. Same-candle target/breakeven collision uses the existing ambiguity policy.
9. An available forward key level uses the key-level target strategy instead.
10. No new entries or trade management continue beyond the applicable 1:00 p.m. ET boundary.
11. Long and short calculations are directionally correct.
12. All calculations use the specific trade’s own entry, stop, risk, and candidate identity.

Do not call the ninth-bar event a breakeven exit. It is only breakeven-stop activation unless price actually fills that stop.

Do not change strategy qualification, patience detection, consolidation handling, pullback tolerance, initial stop calculation, historical indexing, or live/broker behavior.

Because compute is limited, run only focused target-planning, OHLCV execution, Phase 9, and configuration tests. Report files changed, the exact bar-count convention, tests run, and any remaining limitations.
