A. Patience-candle stop range

The stop is a buffered range, not the exact patience-candle extreme.

For a long trade:

strategy stop = patience candle low - 12 MES ticks

For a short trade:

strategy stop = patience candle high + 12 MES ticks

MES tick size is 0.25, so the default 12-tick buffer equals 3.00 points.

Requirements:

- Add a governed setting named `patienceStopBufferTicks`.
- Default it to 12 ticks.
- Preserve the catastrophe stop as a separate control.
- Freeze the calculated strategy stop when the candidate is created.
- Do not move the original stop closer after entry.
- Record:
  - Patience-candle extreme.
  - Buffer ticks and points.
  - Final stop boundary.
  - Direction.
  - Source P candle and audit ID.
- Bump the formula/configuration hash because this changes modeled outcomes.

B. ORB break → pullback → patience → immediate entry

A qualifying pullback may contain one or more completed five-minute candles.

The patience candle may itself be the first and only pullback candle.

Required causal sequence:

1. Finalize the ORB/NTZ from the first three completed five-minute candles.
2. Require a completed directional break outside the ORB.
3. Begin a pullback sequence after the break.
4. Allow the pullback sequence to contain one or more completed candles.
5. Qualify when any pullback candle reaches or comes within the governed 12-tick tolerance of an allowed key level.
6. That same candle may also qualify as P if it satisfies the patience-candle shape.
7. Only the immediate next completed candle may act as E.
8. Long: E must break above P high by the configured entry-confirmation buffer.
9. Short: E must break below P low by the configured entry-confirmation buffer.
10. If immediate E fails, expire P and continue scanning for a later P2→E2 pair.
11. Never let a later candle confirm an expired P.
12. Entry confirmation must complete before 1:00 p.m. ET.

Do not require two or more pullback candles.

Keep these governed settings distinct:

- Pullback proximity: 12 ticks.
- Entry confirmation: existing 3–4 tick setting.
- Stop buffer: 12 ticks.
