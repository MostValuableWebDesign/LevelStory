OBJECTIVE

Change the strategy stop for only:

STRONG_BREAKOUT_AFTER_CONSOLIDATION

After a valid breakout entry, exit the trade when price reenters more than halfway through the same frozen consolidation zone used to qualify that breakout.

The current implementation derives the consolidation breakout stop outside the zone:

- Long: consolidationZoneLow - 8 ticks
- Short: consolidationZoneHigh + 8 ticks

Replace that Strong Breakout stop behavior with the midpoint-reentry rule below.

Do not merely change the displayed chart level. Update the authoritative strategy stop, replay execution, trade results, audit evidence, and Visual Review.

AUTHORITATIVE ZONE

Use the exact frozen causal consolidation zone belonging to the executed breakout occurrence:

- H = frozen consolidationZoneHigh
- L = frozen consolidationZoneLow
- M = L + ((H - L) / 2)
- t = the contract tick size; MES currently uses 0.25

Never recalculate the zone from later candles. The zone must remain frozen after the breakout.

Fail closed when:

- H or L is missing or nonfinite
- H <= L
- The direct consolidation range conflicts with the consolidation guard range
- The tick size is unavailable or invalid
- The candidate, occurrence, audit, and execution do not reference the same frozen zone

LONG RULE

For a long breakout:

1. Entry must still occur above H under the existing Strong Breakout entry rules.
2. After the modeled entry fill, the strategy stop becomes active.
3. The stop is triggered when price trades strictly below M.
4. Merely touching M does not trigger the stop because that is exactly halfway, not more than halfway.
5. Calculate the first valid MES tick strictly below M:

   longStop = (ceil(M / t) - 1) * t

Example:

- Zone: 100.00–104.00
- Midpoint: 102.00
- Long strategy stop: 101.75
- Trade at 102.00: no exit
- Trade at 101.75: execute the strategy stop

SHORT RULE

For a short breakout:

1. Entry must still occur below L under the existing Strong Breakout entry rules.
2. After the modeled entry fill, the strategy stop becomes active.
3. The stop is triggered when price trades strictly above M.
4. Merely touching M does not trigger the stop.
5. Calculate the first valid MES tick strictly above M:

   shortStop = (floor(M / t) + 1) * t

Example:

- Zone: 100.00–104.00
- Midpoint: 102.00
- Short strategy stop: 102.25
- Trade at 102.00: no exit
- Trade at 102.25: execute the strategy stop

Use integer tick arithmetic internally to avoid floating-point drift. Do not round M to a valid tick before applying the strict “more than halfway” rule.

TIMING AND FILL BEHAVIOR

This is an intrabar price stop. A candle does not need to close beyond the midpoint.

- Do not use any part of the entry candle occurring before the modeled entry-fill timestamp to trigger the stop.
- With ordered tick or quote evidence, only post-fill observations may trigger it.
- If a later candle crosses the stop and then closes back in the favorable half, the stop was still hit.
- If a post-entry candle opens beyond the stop, use the opening price or first executable observed price under the existing adverse-gap and slippage rules.
- If price crosses the stop normally, use the calculated stop tick as the reference fill, then apply the existing slippage and fee model.
- If entry and midpoint reentry are both possible within one OHLC candle but ordered intrabar evidence is unavailable, do not invent the sequence. Record the existing event-order ambiguity and apply the current conservative execution policy.
- If the target and strategy stop are both reachable during the same OHLC candle without authoritative ordering, preserve the existing conservative ambiguity policy.
- Do not authorize ordered execution from a nonempty tick array alone. Evidence must match the contract, source, candle interval, and post-entry time range.

OTHER STOP BEHAVIOR

This rule replaces only the ordinary strategy stop for `STRONG_BREAKOUT_AFTER_CONSOLIDATION`.

- Preserve the catastrophe stop as an independent emergency safeguard.
- Preserve existing breakeven and trailing-stop behavior.
- The earliest authoritatively ordered exit event wins.
- If the first observable post-entry price gaps through both the strategy stop and catastrophe stop, use the existing conservative gap-fill and exit-reason precedence. Record both breached levels in the audit evidence.
- Recalculate initial risk and all R-based values from the new midpoint-reentry strategy stop.
- Do not allow an old ordinary-stop or 1R fallback to restore the previous eight-tick-outside stop.
