
Phase 5 — Patience-candle engine
Implement only Phase 5 of LevelStory.

This phase is timing-sensitive. Do not simplify the rules.

PATIENCE-CANDLE LOCATION

A patience candle may form after:

- A pullback reaches or comes within proximity of a qualifying level
- Price consolidates near a qualifying level
- Price completes extended consolidation inside or near the NTZ

PATIENCE-CANDLE DEFINITION

The patience candle must be completely closed.

Initial containment rule:

- Its high is no higher than the previous completed candle’s high
- Its low is no lower than the previous completed candle’s low

The patience candle is not the entry.

IMMEDIATE NEXT-CANDLE RULE

Only the immediately following 5-minute candle can trigger entry.

Bullish:

- Trigger intrabar when the immediate next candle first trades above the patience-candle high

Bearish:

- Trigger intrabar when the immediate next candle first trades below the patience-candle low

Do not wait for the trigger candle to close.

EXPIRATION

If the immediate next candle does not break the patience candle in the intended direction:

- Expire the setup
- Require a completely new patience-candle pattern

A later candle cannot trigger from an expired patience candle.

OPPOSITE-SIDE INVALIDATION

If the immediate next candle first breaks the opposite side:

- Invalidate the setup
- Do not enter if it later crosses the intended side

AMBIGUITY

If both sides are touched and tick or lower-timeframe data cannot prove which occurred first:

- Reject the setup
- Mark it AMBIGUOUS
- Never assume the profitable sequence

STATES

Display:

- WAITING FOR PATIENCE CANDLE
- PATIENCE CANDLE FORMING
- VALID PATIENCE CANDLE
- TRIGGER CANDLE ACTIVE
- ENTRY TRIGGERED
- INVALIDATED
- EXPIRED
- AMBIGUOUS

TESTS

Create deterministic sequences testing:

- Valid bullish pattern
- Valid bearish pattern
- Incomplete patience candle
- Failed immediate trigger
- Later candle incorrectly attempting to trigger
- Bullish opposite-side-first break
- Bearish opposite-side-first break
- Both sides touched
- Gap above or below trigger
- Consolidation patience candle
- Pullback patience candle

Do not proceed until every timing test passes.
