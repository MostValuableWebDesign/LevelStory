



Phase 4 — Breakout, pullback, Fibonacci and volume
Use High effort.
Implement only Phase 4 of LevelStory.

ORB BREAKOUT

A valid initial breakout event requires:

- NTZ is complete
- A completed 5-minute candle closes outside the NTZ
- Breakout direction is recorded
- Distance outside the NTZ is recorded
- Breakout volume is recorded

Do not create an entry directly from this breakout.

PULLBACK

Detect when price subsequently:

- Touches a qualifying level
- Comes within proximity
- Breaks and reclaims it
- Holds it
- Consolidates near it
- Breaks through it

Qualifying levels include:

- NTZ/ORB boundary
- Premarket high/low
- Yesterday’s high/low
- Two-days-ago high/low
- VWAP
- 200 EMA
- Major hourly level
- Fibonacci level
- Confluence level

Initial proximity:

Maximum of:

- Two ticks
- 0.10 of 5-minute 14-period ATR

Initial maximum pullback duration:

- Six completed 5-minute candles
- Thirty minutes

FIBONACCI

For bullish setups:

- Anchor from impulse swing low to breakout swing high

For bearish setups:

- Anchor from impulse swing high to breakout swing low

Freeze anchors once the pullback begins.

Allow manual anchor correction.

Display:

- 0%
- 23.6%
- 38.2%
- 40%
- 50%
- 61.8%
- 78.6%
- 100%

Classify:

- Below 38.2%: shallow
- 38.2%–50%: normal
- 50%–61.8%: deep
- Above 61.8%: elevated failure risk
- 100%: original impulse fully retraced

Do not treat Fibonacci depth alone as proof of reversal.

VOLUME

Use the previous six completed 5-minute candles as the primary volume baseline.

Initial supporting breakout volume:

- At least 1.25 times the baseline

Compare pullback volume with:

- Breakout volume
- Average impulse volume
- Recent six-candle average

Equal or higher opposing volume creates:

HIGH-VOLUME PULLBACK — POSSIBLE REVERSAL

Keep Fibonacci depth and volume strength as separate variables.
