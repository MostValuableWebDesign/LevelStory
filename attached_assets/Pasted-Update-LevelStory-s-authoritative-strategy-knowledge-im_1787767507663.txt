CORE DEFINITION

A patience candle is a completed, trend-aligned candle that forms after a pullback or consolidation at or near an important level.

A patience candle is not necessarily a traditional inside candle.

Only the side facing the established trend must remain inside the corresponding side of the preceding completed candle.

A candle cannot qualify as a bullish or bearish patience candle based on its shape alone. The established trend must agree with the patience-candle direction.

TREND REQUIREMENT

Bullish patience candles are allowed only when the established strategy trend is bullish.

Bearish patience candles are allowed only when the established strategy trend is bearish.

Use the existing LevelStory 15-minute trend classifier.

If the trend is neutral:

- Do not qualify an ordinary patience-candle continuation setup.
- Display WAITING — TREND UNCLEAR.

If the candle points against the established trend:

- Do not label it an ordinary patience candle.
- It may be recorded as possible reversal evidence.
- It must be evaluated by the separate reversal strategy.

BULLISH PATIENCE CANDLE

A valid bullish patience candle requires:

1. Established trend is bullish.
2. A qualified bullish ORB breakout, pullback, or valid bullish consolidation context exists.
3. Price is at or near a qualifying critical level.
4. The candidate candle is completely closed.
5. The candidate candle’s high does not surpass the preceding completed candle’s high.

Use:

bullishPatience =
trend == BULLISH
&& contextIsValid
&& nearQualifyingLevel
&& candidateCandle.isClosed
&& candidateCandle.high <= precedingCandle.high

The bullish patience candle’s low may extend below the preceding candle’s low.

Do not reject a bullish patience candle merely because its bottom wick or body extends below the preceding candle.

BEARISH PATIENCE CANDLE

A valid bearish patience candle requires:

1. Established trend is bearish.
2. A qualified bearish ORB breakout, pullback, or valid bearish consolidation context exists.
3. Price is at or near a qualifying critical level.
4. The candidate candle is completely closed.
5. The candidate candle’s low does not surpass the preceding completed candle’s low.

Use:

bearishPatience =
trend == BEARISH
&& contextIsValid
&& nearQualifyingLevel
&& candidateCandle.isClosed
&& candidateCandle.low >= precedingCandle.low

The bearish patience candle’s high may extend above the preceding candle’s high.

Do not reject a bearish patience candle merely because its upper wick or body extends above the preceding candle.

“DOES NOT SURPASS” DEFINITION

Use the complete candle extreme, including body and wick:

- Bullish comparison uses candle highs.
- Bearish comparison uses candle lows.

Do not compare only candle bodies.

Use the instrument’s exact high and low prices.

IMMEDIATE NEXT-CANDLE REQUIREMENT

Only the immediately following candle can trigger an entry.

A later candle cannot trigger from the same patience candle.

If the immediate next candle does not reach the required entry price before it closes:

- Mark the patience setup EXPIRED.
- Require a new patience candle.
- Do not allow a later candle to reuse the expired pattern.

ENTRY CONFIRMATION BUFFER

Do not enter on the first tick beyond the patience candle.

Require price to surpass the patience candle by a configurable confirmation buffer.

Initial allowed buffer:

- Three ticks
- Four ticks

The trader must select the exact buffer before the setup triggers.

Default shadow-testing value:

- Four ticks

Make the buffer configurable per futures contract.

BULLISH ENTRY

For a valid bullish patience candle:

longEntryPrice =
patienceCandle.high
+ (entryBufferTicks × contractTickSize)

The immediately following candle must trade at or above longEntryPrice.

Enter intrabar when that price is reached.

Example:

patience high = 6000.00
tick size = 0.25
entry buffer = 4 ticks

long entry =
6000.00 + (4 × 0.25)
= 6001.00

BEARISH ENTRY

For a valid bearish patience candle:

shortEntryPrice =
patienceCandle.low
- (entryBufferTicks × contractTickSize)

The immediately following candle must trade at or below shortEntryPrice.

Enter intrabar when that price is reached.

Example:

patience low = 6000.00
tick size = 0.25
entry buffer = 4 ticks

short entry =
6000.00 - (4 × 0.25)
= 5999.00

INSUFFICIENT BREAK

If the next candle crosses the patience high or low but does not reach the complete three-to-four-tick buffer:

- Do not enter.
- Display BREAK DETECTED — WAITING FOR CONFIRMATION BUFFER.
- If the full buffer is not reached before that candle closes, expire the setup.

STOP PLACEMENT

Use a strategy stop beyond the opposite side of the patience candle.

Initial configurable protective buffer:

- One tick beyond the opposite patience-candle extreme

Bullish:

longStrategyStop =
patienceCandle.low
- (stopBufferTicks × contractTickSize)

Bearish:

shortStrategyStop =
patienceCandle.high
+ (stopBufferTicks × contractTickSize)

The hard catastrophe stop must also remain active.

Before authorizing entry:

- Calculate the complete distance from buffered entry to buffered stop.
- Include slippage and fees.
- Calculate futures risk per contract.
- Reduce contract quantity if necessary.
- Reject the trade if even one contract exceeds the maximum permitted risk.

Do not tighten or move the initial stop merely to force the position into the risk limit.

A+ PATIENCE SETUP

Classify a patience setup as A+ only when all required conditions pass:

1. Clear established 15-minute trend.
2. Qualified ORB breakout with strong volume and continuation.
3. Pullback or valid consolidation.
4. Price reaches or comes near a critical level.
5. Critical level has meaningful confluence.
6. Pullback volume supports continuation.
7. Fibonacci context supports the setup.
8. Valid trend-aligned patience candle.
9. Immediate next candle reaches the full three-to-four-tick entry buffer.
10. Spread, liquidity, stale-data, daily-loss, and position-risk checks pass.

Do not classify a setup as A+ based only on the patience-candle shape.

REVERSAL WARNING

If the established trend is bullish:

- Do not classify a red or lower-contained candle as bearish patience merely because its shape appears bearish.

If the established trend is bearish:

- Do not classify a green or upper-contained candle as bullish patience merely because its shape appears bullish.

An opposing pattern may be:

- Possible reversal evidence
- A failed continuation warning
- A level rejection
- A doji or equivalent-candle alert

It must use the separate reversal strategy and cannot become an ordinary patience continuation entry.

REQUIRED SEQUENCE

For a normal continuation trade:

1. Determine established trend.
2. Qualify the ORB breakout.
3. Confirm strong continuation.
4. Wait for pullback or valid consolidation.
5. Confirm price is at or near a critical level.
6. Wait for a completed trend-aligned patience candle.
7. Calculate the buffered entry.
8. Calculate the buffered strategy stop.
9. Calculate risk and allowed contracts.
10. Monitor only the immediately following candle.
11. Enter intrabar only when the complete three-to-four-tick buffer is reached.
12. Expire the setup if that immediate candle fails to reach the entry price.

INTERFACE DEFINITION

Use this plain-language explanation:

“A patience candle is a completed, trend-aligned candle that forms after a pullback or consolidation near an important level. In a bullish trend, its high remains at or below the preceding candle’s high. In a bearish trend, its low remains at or above the preceding candle’s low. Only the immediately following candle can trigger entry, and price must move the configured three-to-four ticks beyond the patience candle before entry.”
