Implement only Phase 11C: statistically honest walk-forward edge validation.

Do not change or automatically optimize the trading formula during this phase.

Goal:
Determine whether the current fixed formula shows positive performance on untouched historical periods after fees and modeled slippage.

1. Dataset partitions
- Divide chronological data into fixed in-sample and untouched out-of-sample windows.
- Never select random future dates for training.
- Display exact dates and contracts in every partition.
- Prevent out-of-sample results from influencing rule configuration.
- Store a hash of the formula configuration used for every run.

2. Walk-forward evaluation
Support multiple chronological folds, for example:
- Earlier period: descriptive/in-sample review
- Following period: untouched validation
- Advance forward and repeat without mixing future data backward

Do not automatically change parameters between folds.

3. Required metrics
Report:
- Trade count
- Win rate
- Average win
- Average loss
- Net expectancy per trade
- Gross and net P&L
- Profit factor
- Maximum drawdown
- Consecutive losses
- Fees
- Slippage
- Ambiguity count
- Rejected-entry count
- Target exits
- Strategy stops
- Catastrophe stops
- Runner exits
- Session-close exits

4. Segmentation
Show results by:
- In-sample/out-of-sample
- Contract
- Month
- Long/short
- Time of day
- Setup type
- Trend
- Volume condition
- Fibonacci depth
- Level/confluence type
- Market regime

Display “insufficient sample” rather than drawing conclusions from very small groups.

5. Edge status
Use neutral classifications:
- Insufficient evidence
- Negative observed expectancy
- Mixed/inconclusive
- Positive observed expectancy requiring further validation

Do not display “proven edge” merely because a small sample is profitable.

Suggested minimum evidence before stronger conclusions:
- At least 100 qualified trades overall
- Meaningful untouched out-of-sample trade count
- Positive net expectancy after costs
- Profit factor above 1 after costs
- Drawdown within the configured risk tolerance
- Results not dependent on one contract, month or direction

These are research thresholds, not guarantees of future profitability.

6. Robustness
Run sensitivity reports using reasonable fee and slippage assumptions, but do not select the best result.

Show:
- Normal cost
- Higher-cost stress case
- Adverse slippage case

7. Tests
Prove:
- Holdout dates remain untouched.
- No future-data leakage.
- Formula hashes remain fixed.
- Fold boundaries are chronological.
- Metrics reconcile with individual trades.
- Segments reconcile with totals.
- Small samples receive insufficient-evidence labels.
- Cached results are deterministic.

Final report
- List files changed.
- Report all verification results.
- Show every fold and date range.
- Clearly state whether evidence is sufficient.
- Do not recommend live trading.
