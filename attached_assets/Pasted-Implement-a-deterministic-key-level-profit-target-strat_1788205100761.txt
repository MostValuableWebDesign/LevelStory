Implement a deterministic key-level profit-target strategy in LevelStory.

Core rule

Take profit at the next eligible key level in the trade’s direction, normally 12 MES ticks before price reaches that level.

MES configuration:

tickSize = 0.25
profitTargetBufferTicks = 12
profitTargetBufferPoints = 3.00

Directional calculation

Long trade:
- Consider only key levels above the entry.
- Target = keyLevelPrice - 3.00 points.

Short trade:
- Consider only key levels below the entry.
- Target = keyLevelPrice + 3.00 points.

This places the target on the near side of the level.

Bypass rule

If the entry price is already within 12 ticks of a key level, bypass that level and search for the next key level farther in the trade direction.

Long:

distance = levelPrice - entryPrice

The level is eligible only when:

distance > 3.00 points

Short:

distance = entryPrice - levelPrice

The level is eligible only when:

distance > 3.00 points

Do not create a target at the entry price, behind the entry, or with less than 12 ticks of initial profit potential.

Level selection

At entry time:

1. Freeze all causally available key levels.
2. Remove duplicates and merge levels belonging to the same zone.
3. Keep only levels ahead of the trade.
4. Sort them by directional distance from entry.
5. Bypass every level within 12 ticks of entry.
6. Select the nearest remaining eligible level.
7. Freeze that level and its derived target for the life of the trade.

Qualifying key levels

Use only established, causally visible levels:

- ORB/NTZ high and low
- Premarket high and low
- Previous-day high and low
- Two-days-ago high and low
- Major support and resistance zones
- Dynamite zones
- VWAP value frozen at entry, when applicable
- 200 EMA value frozen at entry, when applicable

Do not restore Fibonacci levels.

Zone handling

For a price zone rather than one exact price:

Long:
- Use the lower boundary encountered first.
- Target 12 ticks below that boundary.

Short:
- Use the upper boundary encountered first.
- Target 12 ticks above that boundary.

For a dynamite zone, treat the complete light-blue range as one level. Do not create separate targets for every confluence inside it.

Target placement mode

Add a governed configuration:

profitTargetPlacement:
- NEAR_SIDE_12_TICKS — default
- EXACT_LEVEL — optional comparison/backtest mode

NEAR_SIDE_12_TICKS must be the default.

Target progression

Store the ordered eligible levels with the trade:

- skippedLevels
- selectedTargetLevel
- subsequentTargetLevels

Each skipped level must record:

- Level ID and type
- Price or zone boundaries
- Distance from entry
- Reason: ENTRY_WITHIN_12_TICKS

Do not silently discard it.

Multiple contracts

When more than one contract is traded:

- The first profit-taking contract may exit at the selected target.
- Remaining runner contracts may continue toward the next eligible key level.
- Each subsequent target must follow the same 12-tick bypass and near-side placement rules.
- Preserve the existing runner invalidation rules unless separately changed.

Single-contract trades exit fully at the selected target.

Causal integrity

The target plan must be frozen separately for each candidate-owned trade.

Never reuse:

- Another trade’s target
- Another trade’s entry price
- A later chart level that was unavailable at entry
- A session-level mutable target
- A future VWAP or EMA value

Every trade must retain:

- candidateId
- signalOccurrenceId
- entryPrice
- direction
- frozen available levels
- skipped levels
- selected level ID/type
- selected level price or zone
- buffer ticks
- calculated target price

Historical execution

Management begins with the first completed candle after E.

Long:
- Target is reached when a management candle’s high reaches the frozen target.

Short:
- Target is reached when a management candle’s low reaches the frozen target.

Continue using the existing conservative same-candle ambiguity policy when both stop and target are reached in the same candle.

Visual Review

For every filled trade, display:

- Entry line
- Selected key level or zone
- Buffered profit-target line
- “12 ticks before [level name]”
- Any bypassed nearby level with “Skipped: entry within 12 ticks”
- Exit marker
- Realized gross and net P/L

Do not show a target or P/L for an unfilled candidate.

Required tests

Add deterministic long and short tests proving:

1. A level more than 12 ticks away becomes the selected target.
2. A level exactly 12 ticks from entry is bypassed.
3. A level less than 12 ticks away is bypassed.
4. The following eligible level becomes the target.
5. Long targets are 12 ticks below the selected level.
6. Short targets are 12 ticks above the selected level.
7. Levels behind the entry are ignored.
8. Multiple prices within one dynamite zone produce one target.
9. Separate same-session trades freeze independent target plans.
10. VWAP and EMA use their entry-time values and do not move the target later.
11. The E candle is not used to trigger an exit.
12. Entry, target, exit, and P/L propagate consistently through the trade record, outcome replay, report, and Visual Review.
