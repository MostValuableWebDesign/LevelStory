```text
Update LevelStory’s ORB directional-trend logic so the trend can reverse when price later breaks the opposite side of the finalized ORB.

Implement this as a causal state machine used by every strategy that depends on ORB trend direction.

ORB trend states

- NEUTRAL
- BULLISH_ORB_TREND
- BEARISH_ORB_TREND

Initial trend establishment

After the ORB is finalized:

- A completed candle closing above the finalized ORB high by the governed confirmation buffer changes the state to BULLISH_ORB_TREND.
- A completed candle closing below the finalized ORB low by the governed confirmation buffer changes the state to BEARISH_ORB_TREND.
- An intrabar wick outside the ORB does not establish a trend.
- A candle closing inside the ORB does not establish a trend.
- Use the existing ORB breakout confirmation buffer unless the app already has a dedicated governed setting.
- Do not require a minimum candle-body percentage.
- Do not require the close to be within a particular percentage of the candle range.
- Do not require the candle to close above or below VWAP or EMA 200.

Trend reversal

The first ORB break must not permanently lock the directional trend.

While BULLISH_ORB_TREND is active:

- A completed candle closing below the finalized ORB low by the confirmation buffer invalidates the bullish trend.
- At that candle’s close, change the state to BEARISH_ORB_TREND.

While BEARISH_ORB_TREND is active:

- A completed candle closing above the finalized ORB high by the confirmation buffer invalidates the bearish trend.
- At that candle’s close, change the state to BULLISH_ORB_TREND.

A close back inside the ORB does not reverse the trend by itself. The opposite ORB boundary must be broken by a completed candle close.

Causal timing

- A trend transition becomes known only when the confirming candle completes.
- The new trend state becomes effective for strategy evaluation beginning with the following candle.
- Never use the confirming candle’s future high, low, close, indicator values, or subsequent price action before it completes.
- Preserve the finalized ORB high and low for the entire session.
- Do not reconstruct or move the ORB boundaries after a trend reversal.

Strategy-arm behavior

When the ORB trend reverses:

- Expire all unfilled eligibility arms, pending patience sequences, and pending entry confirmations belonging to the previous direction.
- Record their expiration reason as `ORB_TREND_REVERSED`.
- Create a new directional trend epoch for the opposite direction.
- Allow the existing strategy engines to search for new qualifying pullbacks, patience candles, continuation entries, and Early ORB Momentum Continuation setups in the new direction.
- A trend reversal is not automatically an entry.
- The new direction must still satisfy the applicable strategy’s normal entry requirements.
- Do not reuse a patience candle or E candle from the previous trend epoch.
- Do not collapse occurrences from different trend epochs into one canonical candidate.
- Permit multiple reversals during a session when each reversal is independently confirmed.

Active-position behavior

- A trend reversal does not automatically close an existing position unless an existing stop, target, breakeven, recovery, early-failure, or session-close rule requires the exit.
- Continue managing the existing position under its frozen management plan.
- The account-wide single-active-position rule must prevent entry in the new direction while the previous position remains active.
- The app may detect and display the new opposite-direction setup while blocked, but it must not enter it or include it in P/L.
- Once the existing position fully exits, later eligible occurrences from the current trend epoch may enter normally.

Configuration

Add governed settings if equivalent settings do not already exist:

- `orbTrendReversalEnabled: true`
- `orbTrendConfirmationCloses: 1`
- `orbTrendConfirmationBufferTicks`: reuse the existing ORB breakout buffer
- `orbTrendEffectiveTiming: NEXT_CANDLE`
- `orbTrendInsideCloseBehavior: PRESERVE_CURRENT_TREND`
- `orbTrendWickOnlyBehavior: NO_STATE_CHANGE`

Include these settings in formula hashing, governed strategy snapshots, cache keys, candidate projection versions, and audit metadata.

Audit requirements

Record every state transition with:

- Previous trend state
- New trend state
- Trend epoch ID
- Finalized ORB high and low
- Confirmation buffer
- Confirming candle open and close timestamps
- Confirming candle OHLC
- Boundary crossed
- Effective-from timestamp
- Expired arm and candidate IDs
- Expiration reason
- Whether an active position prevented a new entry
- Formula and strategy versions

Visual Review

Display:

- Initial ORB trend establishment
- Every later ORB trend reversal
- The active trend direction at each candidate’s evaluation timestamp
- The candle that confirmed the transition
- When the new trend became effective
- Previous-direction candidates invalidated by the transition
- Opposite-direction candidates detected but blocked by an active position

Required tests

Add deterministic tests covering:

- Upside break establishes a bullish trend.
- Downside break establishes a bearish trend.
- Bullish trend later reverses after a completed close below the ORB low.
- Bearish trend later reverses after a completed close above the ORB high.
- A wick outside the opposite boundary does not reverse the trend.
- A close inside the ORB does not reverse the trend.
- The reversal is unavailable until the confirming candle completes.
- The new state is effective beginning with the following candle.
- Previous-direction pending arms expire on reversal.
- New opposite-direction arms use unique epoch and occurrence identities.
- Multiple confirmed reversals remain independently auditable.
- Same-direction continuation does not create unnecessary new epochs.
- An existing open position continues to be managed after a trend reversal.
- The single-active-position gate blocks an opposite-direction entry until the existing position fully exits.
- Blocked opposite-direction candidates remain visible but are excluded from P/L.
- Historical execution and Shadow Account Replay produce identical transition ordering.
- No future candle data is used.
- Cache and projection versions invalidate stale results.

Run API typechecking, focused ORB state-machine and strategy tests, Phase 9 tests, Visual Review and Shadow Replay tests, frontend typechecking/build, and `git diff --check`. Report exact results and any remaining validation limitations.
```
