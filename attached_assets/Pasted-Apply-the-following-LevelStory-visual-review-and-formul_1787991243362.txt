PART 6 — FIBONACCI ELIGIBILITY RULE

Correction:

Do not calculate, apply, display or use Fibonacci retracement as confluence unless price has first completed a qualifying pullback interaction with a recognized key level.

Recognized key levels:

- ORB / NTZ High or Low
- VWAP
- EMA 200
- Premarket High or Low
- Previous Day High or Low
- Two Days Back High or Low
- validated support or resistance
- validated critical/confluence level

Required order:

1. Valid breakout or directional impulse exists.
2. Price begins a pullback.
3. Pullback reaches or comes within the configured tolerance of a recognized key level.
4. The key-level interaction is causally confirmed.
5. Only then freeze the impulse anchors and calculate Fibonacci retracement.
6. Use Fibonacci as supporting confluence—not as the initial reason for recognizing the pullback.
7. Then wait for the patience candle and immediate-next entry candle.

If no key-level interaction occurred:

- `fibonacciEligible = false`
- do not display Fib levels
- do not use Fibonacci in qualification
- do not record Fibonacci confluence
- explain:
  `Fibonacci not evaluated because no qualifying pullback-to-level interaction occurred.`

If the interaction occurred:

- freeze causal swing anchors
- calculate Fibonacci
- display only the relevant retracement levels
- record the touched key level and retracement percentage
- never recalculate anchors using later candles

This changes formula behavior.

Therefore:

- increment the fixed formula version
- regenerate the formula hash
- invalidate only formula-dependent deterministic caches
- do not rebuild the historical candle index

Add focused strategy tests proving:

- later candles cannot alter frozen anchors
