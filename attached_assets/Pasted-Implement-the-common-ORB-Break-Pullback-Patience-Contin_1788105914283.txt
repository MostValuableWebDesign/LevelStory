Implement the common ORB Break–Pullback–Patience Continuation edge correctly and consistently across all historical MES charts.

EDGE NAME

ORB_BREAK_PULLBACK_PATIENCE_CONTINUATION

CORE PATTERN

The strategy must recognize this causal sequence on completed five-minute candles:

1. The ORB/NTZ is finalized from the first three completed five-minute candles:
   9:30–9:35 ET
   9:35–9:40 ET
   9:40–9:45 ET

2. Price breaks above ORB high for a long or below ORB low for a short.

3. The breakout shows directional continuation.

4. Price retraces toward a qualifying key-level zone.

5. A valid patience candle forms during or after that pullback.

6. The immediate next completed candle confirms the patience candle.

7. The confirmed signal creates one trade and records every supporting confluence.

BULLISH SEQUENCE

- Completed close above ORB high.
- Bullish continuation establishes the local direction.
- Price pulls back toward a qualifying level.
- Bullish patience candle P:
  P.high must not exceed the preceding candle’s high.
- The whole P candle does not need to be inside the previous candle.
- P color is irrelevant.
- Immediate next candle E must trade at least the configured entry buffer above P.high.
- Default entry buffer: 3–4 MES ticks.
- Entry occurs at the buffered breakout price, not at P’s close.

BEARISH SEQUENCE

- Completed close below ORB low.
- Bearish continuation establishes the local direction.
- Price pulls back toward a qualifying level.
- Bearish patience candle P:
  P.low must not fall below the preceding candle’s low.
- The whole P candle does not need to be inside the previous candle.
- P color is irrelevant.
- Immediate next candle E must trade at least the configured entry buffer below P.low.
- Default entry buffer: 3–4 MES ticks.
- Entry occurs at the buffered breakout price, not at P’s close.

PATIENCE RE-ARMING

Only the candle immediately after P may confirm P.

If E does not cross the buffered trend-side extreme:

- Expire that P candidate.
- Do not allow any later candle to confirm it.
- Keep the ORB pullback setup armed if its broader structure is still valid.
- Continue scanning for a new patience candle P2.
- Only the candle immediately after P2 may confirm P2.
- Continue this sequence until confirmed or structurally invalidated.

KEY-LEVEL INTERACTION

Use a default pullback proximity zone of 12 MES ticks, equal to 3.00 points.

Qualifying levels include:

- ORB/NTZ high and low
- VWAP
- 200 EMA on the five-minute chart
- Premarket high and low
- Previous-day high and low
- Day-before-previous-day high and low
- Confirmed support and resistance
- Valid causal Fibonacci retracement levels

Calculate interaction as the distance between the candle’s full high-low range and the level—not merely the candle close.

A level qualifies when it:

- Falls inside the candle’s high-low range; or
- Is within 12 ticks of that range.

For dynamic indicators such as VWAP and 200 EMA:

- Use the indicator value at the exact candle timestamp.
- Never use the final session value to validate an earlier candle.

The qualifying level interaction may occur:

- On an earlier pullback candle L;
- On the patience candle P itself; or
- Across the causal pullback sequence immediately preceding P.

P may equal L. Do not require L and P to be different candles.

If P touches, crosses, rejects, or comes within 12 ticks of VWAP or the 200 EMA, that indicator must be available as a qualifying pullback level.

BREAKOUT QUALITY

A slight ORB probe without continuation must not arm an entry.

If price barely crosses the ORB boundary and immediately stalls or returns inside:

- Record WEAK_ORB_PROBE.
- Wait for a later meaningful directional push.
- Do not permanently reject the session.

A meaningful breakout may be established by existing causal impulse/continuation evidence, such as:

- A decisive completed close beyond ORB;
- Continued price expansion outside ORB; or
- A follow-through candle maintaining the breakout direction.

Volume may strengthen the setup grade, but do not require one rigid volume threshold unless governed by configuration.

Do not require the breakout to satisfy the separate
STRONG_BREAKOUT_AFTER_CONSOLIDATION strategy.

TREND RULE

The ORB breakout and continuation establish this edge’s local causal direction.

The 15-minute trend may add confluence and improve the setup grade, but it must not automatically block an otherwise valid ORB break–pullback–patience sequence.

CONFLUENCE AND GRADING

A confirmed P→E sequence creates one trade.

Supporting evidence increases the grade instead of creating duplicate trades:

- One qualifying level: base grade
- Multiple nearby qualifying levels: stronger grade
- VWAP plus 200 EMA
- ORB retest plus VWAP
- Prior-day level confluence
- Support/resistance confluence
- Favorable volume
- Confirmed 15-minute trend
- Fibonacci confluence when causally valid
