Inspect the previous requirements and current repository before editing. Implement a new setup named:

`EARLY_ORB_MOMENTUM_CONTINUATION`

Purpose:
Capture valid early directional ORB moves when there was no previously established pullback arm. A completed candle outside the finalized ORB may create its own temporary momentum arm, serve as the patience candle, and allow the immediately following candle to confirm entry.

This must be a separate setup path. Do not weaken or change the existing pullback-arm requirements for other continuation setups.

1. Eligibility window

Evaluate the Early ORB Momentum setup only when:

- The ORB/NTZ is finalized.
- The candle occurs after ORB finalization.
- The candidate candle opens before 10:30 AM ET.
- The candle is a completed five-minute candle.
- No Early ORB Momentum trade has already been entered in the same direction for that trading session.

Do not require:

- A previously established pullback arm.
- A pullback interaction.
- Price to be above or below VWAP.
- Price to be above or below the 200 MA.
- A particular VWAP or moving-average slope.
- A minimum candle-body percentage.
- The close to be within a particular portion of the candle.
- A strong-volume classification.
- A specific candle color beyond any existing directionally necessary patience-candle rules.

VWAP, the 200 MA, volume, and other indicators may remain visible as diagnostic context or supporting confluence, but they must not control this setup’s eligibility.

2. Momentum-arm creation

A completed candle creates an Early ORB Momentum Arm when it closes at least one MES tick outside the finalized ORB/NTZ:

Long:

`CandleClose >= FinalizedOrbHigh + 0.25`

Short:

`CandleClose <= FinalizedOrbLow − 0.25`

The same completed candle may simultaneously:

- Establish the Early ORB Momentum Arm.
- Serve as the patience candle.
- Freeze the directional context and entry-time evidence.

This is causal because the arm and patience classification become valid only after that candle closes. No entry may occur inside that candle.

Record:

- Arm ID
- Direction
- Trading date
- ORB high and low
- ORB finalization timestamp
- Arm-creation candle open and close timestamps
- Arm-creation close
- Distance outside the ORB in points and ticks
- Patience-candle high, low, open, and close
- Evaluation timestamp
- Source formula and version

3. Entry confirmation

Only the immediately following five-minute candle may confirm entry.

Use the existing eight-tick confirmation buffer without modification:

Long:

`EntryThreshold = PatienceHigh + 8 MES ticks`

Short:

`EntryThreshold = PatienceLow − 8 MES ticks`

Requirements:

- The entry candle must be the immediately adjacent five-minute candle.
- It must be completed before the trade becomes authoritative.
- Its high or low must actually reach the threshold.
- Do not assume a fill from a later candle.
- Do not enter during the arm-creation/patience candle.
- Preserve the existing conservative OHLCV fill and ambiguity handling.

If the immediately following candle does not reach the threshold, expire that Early ORB Momentum attempt. Do not allow a later candle to reuse it.

4. Invalidation

Invalidate the arm before entry when any of these occurs:

- The immediately following candle fails to reach the buffered entry.
- The next completed candle closes back inside the finalized ORB.
- The existing patience-candle invalidation rules are triggered.
- The entry sequence is ambiguous under existing OHLCV sequencing rules.
- The adaptive stop produces `STOP_DISTANCE_TOO_WIDE`.
- The target plan produces `INSUFFICIENT_REWARD_TO_RISK`.
- The early eligibility window has ended.
- An Early ORB Momentum trade was already entered in that direction during the session.

After expiration, the normal pullback-arm strategy may still create a later independent setup.

5. Stop, target, breakeven, and contracts

Do not create separate management formulas for this setup. Use the existing authoritative management engine.

Stop:

`StopBufferTicks = clamp(ceil(ATR14Ticks × 0.10), 4, 8)`

- Long: patience low − StopBufferTicks
- Short: patience high + StopBufferTicks

Maximum structural risk:

`clamp(ceil(ATR14Ticks × 1.50), 20, 40)`

Reject excessive structural distance with `STOP_DISTANCE_TOO_WIDE`.

Profit target:

`TargetBufferTicks = clamp(ceil(ATR14Ticks × 0.05), 1, 2)`

- Use the nearest eligible profitable directional key level within 20 ticks.
- Place the target before the level’s near side.
- Maximum target: 1.50R.
- One-contract minimum: 0.75R.
- Two-contract minimum: 0.50R.
- Use `INSUFFICIENT_REWARD_TO_RISK` for an obstructing major level.
- Use 1R when no eligible level exists and there is no rejection.

Contract management:

- One contract exits fully at the key-level target or 1R.
- Two contracts exit one at the key-level target or 1R and manage one runner.
- Preserve the existing runner-breakeven timing and confirmed-swing trailing.
- Starting balance must not affect eligibility or management.
