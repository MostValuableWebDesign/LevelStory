Implement Part 3: versioned analysis and strategy-result caching over the existing session catalog and market data.

Inspect existing formula hashes, result caches, and review-set provenance. Extend them consistently.

Use separate dependency-aware caches for:
1. Causal market features, where reusable.
2. Strategy evaluation results: occurrences, candidates, rejection reasons, funnel results, and execution evidence.

Cache identity must include all inputs that affect the output, including:
- Source fingerprints for the session AND required lookback data.
- Contract, session/calendar settings, and timeframe.
- Feature calculation versions and parameters.
- Strategy/formula version and effective configuration.
- Candidate-projection and execution-management versions.
- Execution mode, slippage, fees, sizing, and other applicable inputs.
- Initial state where evaluation depends on it.

Rules:
- Same source and same effective rules: reuse results.
- Changed strategy rules: recompute affected derived results from cached market data.
- Changed level/indicator logic: invalidate dependent features and strategy results.
- Previously empty sessions must be reevaluated under new rules; do not search only dates that previously had trades.
- Distinguish a successfully completed zero-trade result from missing, failed, or incomplete computation.
- Write complete results atomically and deduplicate concurrent equivalent jobs.
- Preserve old results with their original provenance.
- Never label an old formula’s results as current.

Do not assume authoritative executions are independent per day. Respect cross-session state, lookbacks, and active positions. Cache such outputs only with their complete state dependencies.

Required tests:
- Unchanged requests reuse results.
- Rule changes can add or remove qualified setups.
- Zero-trade sessions are reconsidered after rule changes.
- Source/lookback changes invalidate affected results.
- Partial or failed jobs cannot become valid cache hits.
- Different execution settings cannot share incompatible results.

Report cache keys, invalidation rules, and tests. Stop after Part 3.
