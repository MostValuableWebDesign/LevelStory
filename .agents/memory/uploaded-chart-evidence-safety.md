name: Uploaded chart evidence safety
description: Uploaded chart analysis must validate bytes, stop at a causal entry cutoff, and keep pixel-derived exits unscored.
---

Uploaded chart candidates are evidence-backed Shadow Mode projections, not authoritative historical fills. Validate the stored bytes server-side, preserve machine extraction separately from reviewer corrections, freeze setup qualification at the completed entry-decision candle, and leave monetary P/L unscored unless exit and accounting evidence is authoritative.

**Why:** A browser MIME label, a later candle, or a visually estimated exit can silently turn an uncertain chart into fabricated trade performance.

**How to apply:** Keep upload security, causal evaluation, duplicate identity, reviewer confirmation, and Combined Replay eligibility as separate gates; never let a later exit image retroactively qualify or price the setup.
