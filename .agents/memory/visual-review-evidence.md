---
name: Visual review evidence
description: Durable rules for keeping visual-validation samples causally trustworthy and review exports unambiguous.
---

Visual-validation category labels must be gated by structured evidence and exact causal identifiers, not by descriptive text alone. Optional identifiers may be absent on one side of a join, but at least one shared exact causal anchor must prove a unique trade association.

**Why:** Broad date/setup joins and prose-only gates allowed unrelated outcomes or weak evidence to appear as valid review samples.

**How to apply:** Keep machine-visible annotations at or before the evaluation cursor, mark later outcome events as human-only, and keep complete review exports separate from the incorrect/uncertain discrepancy subset.

Raw simulated fixtures may legitimately contain repeated or unusually narrow candle shapes; the visual review should flag that provenance without altering OHLC values.

**Why:** Smoothing or normalizing repetitive fixture candles would make the chart look more realistic while breaking the audit trail between generated bytes and the rendered evidence.

**How to apply:** Preserve raw OHLC coordinates, show the source label, and surface a non-blocking repetitive-fixture warning when the input itself is repetitive.
