---
name: Key-level target plans
description: Deterministic MES target selection freezes causal levels and near-side placement per candidate.
---

Key-level profit targets must be selected from the causal snapshot available at entry. A forward allowlisted level qualifies when its executable price is within the 20 MES-point search range and at least 1R; there is no maximum-R target cap. The eight-MES-tick near-side placement applies only to major/support/resistance levels and VWAP/EMA200 indicators; other eligible structural levels use their raw directional boundary. For a ranged level, use the near boundary when entry is outside the zone and the far boundary when entry is already inside it. Levels below 1R are skipped so search continues outward; no eligible survivor falls back to exactly modeled 1R, never an eight-tick-short 1R target. Persisted plans from an older target-search version are stale and must be regenerated. Missing ATR or source provenance must fail/report explicitly rather than silently using legacy defaults or fabricated timestamps.

**Why:** A shared session target or a later chart level can make two same-session occurrences resolve differently from the evidence that was actually available when each entry became eligible.

**How to apply:** Freeze the allowlisted inputs with source audit/cursor provenance, keep target-plan identity alongside candidate-owned management, and propagate it into modeled trade audit and Visual Review; never let legacy target fields replace an already-frozen candidate plan or bypass replay stale checks. Historical audits may derive ATR14 from their causal completed-candle window when the pullback diagnostic is absent; if that window is genuinely insufficient, reject the candidate explicitly rather than aborting the worker or applying a legacy buffer.
